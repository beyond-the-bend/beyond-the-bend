# Workshop Venue Rental Market Analysis

Created June 17, 2026

## Research Scope

This research looked at workshop, yoga, wellness, retreat, community, and event space rental rates in the Edmonton, Sherwood Park, and nearby retreat venue market.

The goal was to understand what facilitators are currently paying to rent spaces for workshops, day retreats, wellness events, and overnight retreat containers.

Rising Moon Studio was treated as the anchor comparison because Laura teaches there, knows the property, and the retreat centre opportunity being explored may be directly comparable or stronger if the house, land, horses, hot tub, and sauna are developed well.

## Biggest Finding

Rising Moon Studio publishes very clear rental pricing.

For day style workshop rentals, Rising Moon charges:

```text
4 hour event:
$360

6 hour event:
$480

8 hour event:
$600
```

For overnight retreat rentals, Rising Moon charges:

```text
24 hour rental:
$1,500

31 hour rental:
$1,800

48 hour rental:
$2,800
```

This is a very important benchmark.

It means a nature based wellness property near Sherwood Park can charge $600 for an 8 hour workshop rental and $2,800 for a full weekend retreat rental, before the facilitator sells tickets to participants.

## Other Market Comparisons

### Yogalife Studios Edmonton

Yogalife rents studio space for workshops, trainings, sound baths, breathwork sessions, meditation circles, private group classes, wellness practitioner sessions, small team gatherings, corporate offsites, and community events.

Their public page does not list exact rates. It says rates depend on room, location, and booking duration, and that they send a quote after inquiry.

Strategic takeaway:

Yogalife confirms that yoga studios are actively renting space for exactly the kinds of events Laura or outside facilitators may run. However, it is a city studio, not a retreat property. It does not offer land, horses, overnight lodging, sauna, hot tub, or fire circle.

### Peerspace Edmonton Yoga Studio Data

Peerspace lists Edmonton yoga studio rentals and states that yoga studios in Edmonton average approximately:

```text
$238 per hour
```

Visible examples on Peerspace included:

```text
Strong Coffee Studios:
60 guests
$250 per hour

The Shop:
75 guests
$200 per hour

Creative space:
25 guests
$150 per hour

Lakeside retreat style space:
20 guests
$350 per hour
```

Peerspace also states that most yoga studio bookings in Edmonton are scheduled for about 6 hours.

Strategic takeaway:

This suggests that private urban venues can command high hourly rates, especially for creative, event, or corporate use. Rising Moon’s $600 for 8 hours is actually reasonable compared with an hourly marketplace, especially given the wellness amenities and nature setting.

### The Event Space

The Event Space publishes rates for event rooms in the Edmonton and Sherwood Park area.

Observed pricing includes:

```text
Small weekend room starting at:
$270 to $375

Small weekday room starting at:
$140 to $170

Combined larger space:
Weekend half day:
$1,200

Weekend full day:
$1,750

Weekday half day:
$950

Weekday full day:
$1,200
```

Their larger combined space can hold up to 125 people.

Strategic takeaway:

Event venues can charge substantially more than wellness studios when capacity is larger. However, this is a city event space, not a retreat property. The new retreat property would be competing more on atmosphere, land, privacy, and wellness value than pure capacity.

### Orange Hub

Search result snippets from the City of Edmonton show Orange Hub facility rentals around:

```text
$34 per hour

$226 per day for some rooms
```

Examples include meeting rooms, studios, and community oriented spaces.

Strategic takeaway:

Orange Hub represents the low cost public facility end of the market. It is not a direct competitor for a premium retreat property. It does show that basic rooms can be rented cheaply, so the retreat property must clearly sell the experience, not just square footage.

### Integrity Seminars

Integrity Seminars rents space for workshops, seminars, meditation classes, private coaching rooms, and other events.

Their public page says rooms are available by the hour, half day, full day, and multiday. It mentions kitchen access, sound system, and microphone, but does not publish exact prices.

Strategic takeaway:

This confirms that workshop and seminar space rental is an existing local market. The new retreat property would offer a much more sensory and nature based version of this.

### Community Hall Rentals

Search result snippets for Edmonton community halls showed examples such as:

```text
Bannerman Community Hall:
$350 per day for members
$450 per day for non members
```

Strategic takeaway:

Community halls are inexpensive and practical. They are not direct competitors for premium restorative, equine, or retreat experiences, but they are relevant for facilitators with lower budgets.

## Market Layers

The market appears to have several clear layers.

### Low Cost Functional Rental

Examples:

```text
Community halls
Orange Hub
Basic meeting rooms
```

Typical range:

```text
$200 to $450 per day
```

These spaces are useful but not emotionally compelling.

### Wellness Studio Rental

Examples:

```text
Yogalife
City yoga studios
Meditation rooms
Breathwork or sound bath spaces
```

Typical range:

```text
$150 to $250 plus per hour on marketplace listings
Custom quote on some studio websites
```

These spaces are warmer and more aligned with yoga or wellness, but usually lack land, overnight lodging, and retreat atmosphere.

### Premium Event Venue

Examples:

```text
The Event Space
Larger private venues
Corporate event spaces
```

Typical range:

```text
$950 to $1,750 for larger half day or full day rentals
```

These can hold more people but may not have a restorative or nature based identity.

### Nature Based Retreat Venue

Best local example:

```text
Rising Moon Studio
```

Observed rates:

```text
$360 for 4 hours
$480 for 6 hours
$600 for 8 hours
$1,500 for 24 hours
$1,800 for 31 hours
$2,800 for 48 hours
```

This is the most relevant category for the retreat centre opportunity.

Rising Moon proves that a local nature based wellness venue can rent workshop and retreat space successfully at meaningful rates.

The new property could likely charge more than Rising Moon eventually if it can offer:

```text
More land

More privacy

More sleeping capacity

Horses

Hot tub

Sauna

Cold plunge

Fire circle

Beautiful photography

Strong guest experience

Clear contracts and clean booking systems
```

The strongest opportunity is not to compete with cheap halls. It is to become a premium nature based retreat property for facilitators who want to host an experience that feels beautiful, restorative, and already held by the land.

## Cautions

The pricing above is based on published and visible market examples, but it is still not a profit forecast.

Before setting final pricing, confirm:

```text
Zoning

Insurance

Parking

Washroom capacity

Sleeping capacity

Fire safety

Septic and water capacity

Cleaning time

Laundry cost

Guest turnover

Owner split

Damage deposits

Cancellation policy

Whether food service is included or separate
```
