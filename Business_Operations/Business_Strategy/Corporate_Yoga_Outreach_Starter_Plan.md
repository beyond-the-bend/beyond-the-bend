# Workplace Wellness Outreach Starter Plan

Created May 27, 2026

## Why This Makes Sense

Workplace wellness is a strong fit for Beyond the Bend because Laura has already taught corporate yoga before, and her work now includes more than standard yoga classes. It does not require a new certification, a complicated online funnel, or a large content library. It requires a clear offer, a simple message, and consistent outreach to companies that already have wellness budgets.

This can support the bigger business direction by adding higher value work alongside weekly classes and private somatic sessions.

## The Simple Offer

Workplace wellness sessions for teams that want to support stress relief, focus, mobility, and nervous system steadiness.

Sessions can be offered in person or online.

Possible formats:

1. Lunch hour yoga.
2. Gentle desk based movement.
3. Stress relief and breathwork.
4. Restorative yoga for workplace burnout.
5. Mindfulness and meditation.
6. Journaling and guided reflection.
7. Women in leadership wellness sessions.
8. Custom wellness workshops for staff development days.

## What The Work Can Include

The work can shift depending on the group, the space, and the intention for the session.

Possible elements include:

1. Restorative yoga.
2. Gentle somatic movement.
3. Breathwork.
4. Mindful movement.
5. Meditation.
6. Journaling.
7. Guided reflection.
8. Jin Shin Do inspired self care or acupressure education where appropriate.
9. Simple ritual or seasonal reflection.
10. Journeying or shamanic inspired practices in settings where that language is clearly welcome.

For corporate settings, lead with accessible language such as stress relief, grounding, restorative practice, breath, mindfulness, reflection, and body awareness. More spiritual language can be reserved for groups that specifically want that depth.

## Best First Positioning

A grounded workplace wellness session that helps staff release stress, move gently, breathe more fully, reflect, and return to the day feeling clearer and more settled.

This should feel professional, accessible, and calm. It should not sound overly spiritual or too therapeutic for a workplace setting.

## Starter Pricing

These are starting points, not permanent prices.

1. Sixty minute workplace yoga or mindfulness session: $300.
2. Seventy five minute session: $375.
3. Ninety minute workshop: $500.
4. Two hour stress relief workshop: $650.
5. Half day workplace wellness session: $1,200 and up.

If travel, props, custom planning, or a larger group is involved, the rate should increase.

## What To Ask Before Booking

1. How many people do you expect?
2. Is the session in person or online?
3. What kind of space is available?
4. Will participants have mats, chairs, or both?
5. Are there mobility concerns to keep in mind?
6. Is the session meant to be relaxing, educational, energizing, or team building?
7. Who is the main contact for payment and scheduling?

## First Week Outreach Plan

Choose ten organizations and send a warm message.

Suggested rhythm:

1. Send five emails on Monday.
2. Send five emails on Tuesday.
3. Follow up one week later.
4. Track every contact in a simple spreadsheet.
5. If someone replies with interest, send the offer menu and suggest a short call.

## Tracking Columns

1. Company.
2. Contact name.
3. Email.
4. Date sent.
5. Follow up date.
6. Response.
7. Notes.
8. Status.

## Main Reminder

This does not need to become complicated. Workplace wellness can begin with a simple email, a clear rate, and a session Laura already knows how to teach.

The goal is not to build a perfect corporate wellness brand first. The goal is to start conversations with organizations that already understand the value of staff wellness.
