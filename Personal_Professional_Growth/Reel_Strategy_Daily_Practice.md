# Reel Strategy: The Daily Practice

To reach your goal of one reel a day without being on camera, we will follow a simple, sustainable system. This system focuses on your strength: the quiet, grounded wisdom of Beyond the Bend.

### The Three Part Formula

Every reel you post will consist of three layers. 

1. **The Visual (The Peace):** A 5 to 10 second clip of a quiet space. This could be light hitting the floor, a candle flame, or a stack of yoga bolsters.
2. **The Text (The Wisdom):** A single, powerful sentence taken from your writing. 
3. **The Audio (The Vibe):** A soft, trending instrumental track that feels like a deep breath.

### Your First Seven Days

Here is a plan to get you started. We will pull these from your existing brand voice and reflections.

*   **Day 1:** Visual of a cup of tea. Text: "You are allowed to stop before you are exhausted."
*   **Day 2:** Visual of sunlight on a yoga mat. Text: "Coming home to yourself is the only work that matters today."
*   **Day 3:** Visual of a stack of blankets. Text: "Quiet is not the absence of noise. It is the presence of you."
*   **Day 4:** Visual of your hands resting on your lap. Text: "Your worth is not measured by your productivity."
*   **Day 5:** Visual of a window with trees outside. Text: "The world can wait for an hour. You have waited long enough."
*   **Day 6:** Visual of a flickering candle. Text: "Trust the timing of your own unwinding."
*   **Day 7:** Visual of a slow movement (no face). Text: "You do not need to be fixed. You just need to be felt."

### The Batching System

To keep this from feeling like a chore, we will use a "Batching Day" once a week.

*   **Filming:** Every Friday after your last class, spend 15 minutes filming 10 short clips of the studio. 
*   **Writing:** We will look through your Substack drafts together and pick 7 sentences.
*   **Assembly:** Use a simple app like Canva or Instagram itself to add the text over the video. 

This plan protects your energy while building your community. You are sharing your light through your words while remaining the safe, quiet anchor in the background.
