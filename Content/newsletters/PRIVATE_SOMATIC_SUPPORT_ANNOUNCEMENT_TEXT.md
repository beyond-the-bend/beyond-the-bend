I wanted to share something a little more personally today, because it feels like an important new doorway in my work.

Over the past while, I have been feeling called to offer more one to one support for women who are feeling overwhelmed, exhausted, disconnected from their bodies, or in a season of transition. This has been unfolding slowly, and I am beginning to see how much my years of yoga, meditation, somatic study, restorative practice, breathwork, nervous system awareness, and reflective inquiry have been preparing me for this kind of work.

I have recently begun working privately with clients in this way, and I can feel how meaningful this container can be. There is something powerful about having a quiet, steady space where nothing needs to be performed, rushed, or solved immediately. A place where the body can be listened to. A place where a woman can begin to feel herself again.

This work is for women who may be carrying too much, moving through change, recovering from burnout, navigating grief, caregiving, divorce, perimenopause, identity shifts, or simply feeling far away from themselves.

Each session is shaped around what is present. The work may include gentle movement, restorative yoga, breath awareness, body awareness, journaling, reflective conversation, meditation, nervous system support, and quiet integration.

It is not therapy, and it is not medical treatment. It is a grounded, body based, reflective space to help you soften survival patterns, listen inward, and begin to return to yourself with more steadiness and care.

I am opening a small number of founding client spaces for this new private work. The container includes eight weekly private sessions, sixty minutes each. The founding client rate is $800 total, with a payment option of $100 per week for eight weeks.

If this sounds like something your body has been asking for, you can reply to this email and we can begin with a complimentary initial consultation. There is no pressure. The first conversation is simply a chance to talk through what you are looking for, what kind of support would feel useful, and whether this feels like the right fit.

And if someone comes to mind as you read this, someone who may be longing for this kind of support, you are welcome to forward this along.

I am beginning this quietly and intentionally, with only a few spaces, because I want the work to have enough room to be held well. My hope is that this becomes a steady place for women who are ready to stop carrying everything alone and begin listening to the wisdom that has been waiting underneath the noise.

If this speaks to you, just reply and tell me a little about what you are looking for.

With warmth and gratitude,

Laura
