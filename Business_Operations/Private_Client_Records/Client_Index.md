## Standard Client Record

Each client folder may contain:

1. A client overview and evolving support plan.
2. Initial consultation notes.
3. Intake information.
4. A separate note for every completed session.
5. A plan for the next session.
6. Important consent, scope, safety, and referral considerations.
7. Administrative communication related to the private work.

## Privacy

These files contain sensitive personal information. They should remain private, should not be shared casually, and should not be placed in public website or content folders.
