## Possible First Session Opening Words

Jamie, I want to start by saying that we do not need to go into the whole story today. We can let your body lead slowly. The intention is not to force anything open, but to create enough support that your system can begin to feel what is here with less bracing.

At any point, we can pause, change, or stop. You do not have to explain perfectly. We can simply notice what feels supportive and what does not.

## Possible First Session Flow

1. Arrival and grounding, five to ten minutes

Sit or lie down comfortably. Invite Jamie to notice the room, the floor, the support under her body, and one or two neutral or pleasant sensations.

2. Gentle check in, five to ten minutes

Ask what is most present today in her body, energy, and emotions. Keep the question broad and non invasive.

3. Supported restorative posture, fifteen to twenty minutes

Choose a highly supported posture that gives the chest, shoulders, belly, and nervous system a sense of being held rather than exposed.

4. Simple body awareness, five to ten minutes

Invite her to notice support, weight, breath, or temperature. Avoid asking her to go directly into pain unless she naturally brings it forward and seems resourced.

5. Gentle movement or release, five to ten minutes

Offer small movements for shoulders, jaw, hands, feet, or spine if appropriate.

6. Closing integration, five to ten minutes

Ask what felt supportive, what did not, and what she wants to remember from the session.
