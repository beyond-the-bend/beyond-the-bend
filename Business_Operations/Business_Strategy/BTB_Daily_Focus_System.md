# Beyond the Bend Daily Focus System

Updated May 1, 2026

## Purpose

This system exists so Laura does not have to hold the whole business in her head every morning.

The goal is to turn the big project into one clear day of work at a time.

## Current Reality

There are four active work streams.

1. Sanctuary membership
2. Video editing and membership content
3. Substack publishing
4. Marketing and audience growth

These are connected, but they cannot all be the top priority at once.

## Primary Priority

The first priority is to get the Sanctuary membership usable enough that people can begin using it.

This matters because it creates a real offer.

Without a usable offer, marketing creates pressure.

With a usable offer, marketing creates movement.

## Priority Order

### 1. Sanctuary Launch Readiness

This is the main revenue foundation.

The question each week is:

What must be true for a real person to join, log in, and use the Sanctuary without confusion?

Focus areas:

1. Membership structure
2. Access flow
3. First usable library
4. Clear welcome experience
5. Payment or registration path
6. Simple explanation of what members receive

### 2. Video Backlog

Videos matter because they fill the Sanctuary.

The goal is not perfect production.

The goal is a calm, usable library that supports students.

Focus areas:

1. Choose the next video
2. Edit only what is needed
3. Export
4. Save in the correct place
5. Add title and short description
6. Mark complete

### 3. Substack

Substack is the public teaching home.

It builds relationship while the membership becomes ready.

For now, Substack should stay simple.

Focus areas:

1. One post at a time
2. One image per post
3. One short note to share the post
4. One gentle pathway back to the website or Sanctuary

### 4. Marketing

Marketing becomes easier once there is something ready to offer.

Until then, marketing should be light.

Focus areas:

1. Share useful reflections
2. Let people know what is coming
3. Invite interest
4. Avoid a big launch push until the Sanctuary can receive people

## Night Before Planning Rhythm

Each evening, the next day should be planned in a small and realistic way.

Laura gives:

1. Tomorrow schedule
2. Available work windows
3. Energy level
4. Any fixed appointments or teaching times
5. Anything urgent

Codex gives back:

1. One main priority
2. Two smaller support tasks
3. A simple time map
4. A stopping point
5. What not to touch tomorrow

## Default Daily Shape

If the day is busy, use this shape.

1. Fifteen minutes to choose the one most useful task
2. Forty five to ninety minutes on Sanctuary or video work
3. Fifteen minutes to update the tracker or notes
4. Stop before the work becomes foggy

If the day has more space, use this shape.

1. Ninety minutes on Sanctuary launch readiness
2. Break
3. Sixty to ninety minutes on one video
4. Break
5. Thirty minutes on Substack or admin

If the day is low energy, use this shape.

1. Pick one file, one video, or one post
2. Do only the next visible step
3. Leave a note for where to continue

## Weekly Rhythm

### Monday

Plan the week and choose the most important Sanctuary outcome.

### Tuesday

Membership structure or website work.

### Wednesday

Video editing and membership content.

### Thursday

Substack post, image, or publishing tracker.

### Friday

Review, tidy, and choose next week priority.

This rhythm can change around teaching and life.

## Definition Of Progress

Progress is not doing everything.

Progress is making one part of the business more usable, visible, or ready.

Good daily outcomes include:

1. One video finished
2. One Sanctuary section clarified
3. One page improved
4. One Substack post prepared
5. One publishing decision made
6. One confusing pile turned into a list

## What To Ignore For Now

Until the Sanctuary has a usable first version, do not spend major energy on:

1. Big launch campaigns
2. Perfect branding
3. Complicated funnels
4. Full Substack monetization
5. Hiring plans
6. Large redesigns

These can come later.

## Immediate Next Step

The next best move is to create a Sanctuary launch checklist and a Substack publishing tracker.

Then each evening plan tomorrow from those two lists.
