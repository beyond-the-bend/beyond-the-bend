# Sacred Journey Full Production Campaign

## Campaign Purpose

This campaign invites local students into The Sacred Journey: From Crown to Root, a three hour Somatic Restorative Yoga and Reiki workshop with Laura Harvey and Zorana Michalsky at Rising Moon Studio on Tuesday, July 28, 2026.

The goal is to fill the remaining spaces without making the invitation feel pressured, loud, or overly promotional. The campaign should feel like a warm hand on the shoulder, reminding people that they are allowed to rest, listen, and be supported.

## Campaign Goal

1. Fill the remaining workshop spaces.

2. Make the workshop feel intimate, special, grounded, and trustworthy.

3. Help people understand the crown to root structure without turning the campaign into a chakra lesson.

4. Keep the language aligned with Beyond the Bend, warm, reflective, plain spoken, and human.

5. Use the existing Sacred Journey image as the main recognition image while balancing it with quieter support graphics.

## Campaign Positioning

The campaign should not lead with chakra education. It should lead with the felt need.

Many people are carrying too much in the mind, the shoulders, the jaw, the belly, and the places no one else can see. The Sacred Journey offers one evening where they do not have to solve themselves. They can settle into support, breathe, listen, receive Reiki, journal, sip tea, and return to the steadier wisdom of the body.

The chakra system is the map. The body is the doorway. Rest is the invitation.

## Core Message

You do not need to fix yourself to come. You do not need to know the chakra system. You only need a willingness to slow down, listen, and be supported.

## Voice Rules

1. Use complete, natural paragraphs for captions.

2. Do not use exact remaining space counts in public content.

3. Do not use pressure language such as hurry, last chance, limited time only, or do not miss out.

4. Do not promise healing, clearing, balancing, transformation, or emotional release.

5. Use grounded language such as rest, quiet, breath, support, listening, settling, returning, held, and coming home to the body.

6. Keep spiritual language reverent but simple.

7. Do not use dashes in public copy.
