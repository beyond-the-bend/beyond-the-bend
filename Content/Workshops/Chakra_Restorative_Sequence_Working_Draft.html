Beyond the Bend Yoga

Moving Through the Body's Sacred Architecture

A Two Hour Restorative Journey Through the Seven Chakras
7 Chakras 2 hrs Total 17 min Per Chakra Full Props Required
Welcome your students into stillness before you begin. Allow 3 to 5 minutes for settling, bodies arranged, props gathered, eye pillows placed. Let the room breathe before the journey begins. This sequence moves upward through the energy body, from earth to sky, from the held to the released.
1
Muladhara
Root Chakra
Safety, belonging, the right to exist
17 min Total
Red Element: Earth
Supported Constructive Rest Supta Savasana variation
8 min
Supine. Bolster or rolled blanket beneath knees to release the lumbar. Blanket under head. Eye pillow on. Arms slightly away from body, palms up. Feet hip width, letting legs fall open naturally.
Feel the floor receiving you. You do not have to hold yourself up, not here, not now. Let your weight be an offering to the ground beneath you.
Supported Child's Pose Balasana
7 min
Bolster lengthwise between thighs, belly and chest draped over it. Knees wide, big toes touching, or a blanket between the bolster and the floor for height. Arms folded beneath forehead or reaching forward. Blocks under bolster if needed for height.
This is the oldest shape the body knows. Curled, held, close to the earth. You came into the world small and safe. Return here now.
Slowly roll to one side before transitioning
Legs Up the Wall Viparita Karani, brief
2 min
Optional transition pose. Folded blanket beneath sacrum, legs resting up the wall or on seat of a chair. A grounding bridge before moving upward.
Let your roots hold you as we begin to rise.
2
Svadhisthana
Sacral Chakra
Feeling, flow, creative life force
17 min Total
Orange Element: Water
Supported Reclined Butterfly Supta Baddha Konasana
10 min
Bolster under spine lengthwise from sacrum to head. Soles of feet together, knees falling open. Blocks or rolled blankets beneath each thigh for full support. Blanket over hips and belly. Eye pillow on.
The hips hold so much of what we have not let ourselves feel. You do not have to open. You simply have to stop bracing. Let the props do the holding.
Supported Side Lying with Hip Opening
3 min each side
Fetal position on one side. Bolster between knees and shins, a blanket pillow for the head. Allow the top knee to drop toward the bolster, creating a gentle external rotation of the hip. Swap sides.
Water does not fight its container. It finds the shape of whatever holds it. Let your body be fluid here.
3
Manipura
Solar Plexus Chakra
Will, identity, personal authority
17 min Total
Yellow Element: Fire
Supported Prone Rest
8 min
Belly down. Bolster or folded blankets beneath the abdomen and lower ribs, enough to decompress the low back but keep the belly softly pressed. Arms folded, forehead resting on hands or turned to one side. Blanket over the back if desired.
The belly is where we brace, against criticism, against being too much, against being seen. Let the floor be firm enough that you do not have to be. Rest your fire here.
Supported Sphinx gentle backbend
5 min
From prone, place forearms on floor or on a bolster for more height, elbows under shoulders. Chest gently lifts. Blanket beneath hip bones. This is passive. The arms support and there is no muscular effort in the back.
Power does not mean force. This gentle opening at the solar plexus is an invitation to remember your own authority, the kind that does not need to prove itself.
Press back to Child's Pose briefly before transitioning forward
Transition Rest brief Child's Pose
3 min
Return to supported Child's Pose on the bolster. A palate cleanser between the solar plexus and the heart.
We pause between the doing and the feeling. Between the fire and the opening.
4
Anahata
Heart Chakra
Love, grief, the capacity to receive
17 min Total
Green Element: Air
Supported Heart Opener over Bolster
10 min
Bolster placed horizontally across the mat beneath the shoulder blades. Blocks under each end of bolster if height is needed. Head supported by a folded blanket. Legs extended or soles of feet together in butterfly. Arms open to the sides, a T shape or cactus, with blankets supporting the arms if needed. Eye pillow on.
The chest is where we guard ourselves. The armoring we learned before we knew we were learning it. This shape does not ask you to be unguarded. It simply asks you to notice the armor, and to breathe beneath it.
Supported Seated Forward Fold Paschimottanasana
7 min
Seated with legs extended. Bolster across shins. Fold forward, resting the torso on the bolster, forehead down. Strap around feet if needed to lengthen hamstrings without strain. Blanket under sitting bones if needed.
After opening, we bow. The heart that opens must also know how to be tender, how to turn inward with the same care it offers outward.
5
Vishuddha
Throat Chakra
Voice, truth, the right to speak
17 min Total
Blue Element: Sound
Supported Fish Pose Matsyasana
10 min
Bolster lengthwise under the spine, blocks under the bolster to create a steeper incline if desired. Head drops back gently. A blanket roll beneath the neck for support, or the back of the head resting on the mat. Legs extended or in butterfly. Arms wide. The throat opens upward.
So much of what has never been said lives here, in the throat, behind the sternum, in the jaw we have set against speaking. You do not have to speak now. But notice what rises when there is finally room for it.
Supported Seated Neck Release
4 min
Seated in a chair or on a folded blanket. Right hand holds the left side of the chair seat or rests on the floor. Left ear drops toward left shoulder. Gentle traction, no forcing. Hold. Swap sides.
The throat and the jaw are where we carry the words we swallowed. Let the weight of the head do the work. You are practicing the art of letting go without effort.
Supported Reclined Twist brief
3 min
Supine. Knees drawn to chest, then drop to one side onto a bolster or folded blankets. Arms open, gaze opposite direction or soft. A brief wringing out before rising into the upper chakras.
Let the body release what the voice has not yet found words for.
6
Ajna
Third Eye Chakra
Intuition, inner seeing, stillness
17 min Total
Indigo Element: Light
Supported Wide Legged Forward Fold Upavistha Konasana
8 min
Seated, legs spread wide. Bolster on blocks lengthwise in front of the body. Fold forward, resting the forehead on the bolster, or on stacked blocks and a blanket. The forehead should find contact with something. Arms relaxed forward or to the sides.
The third eye does not see outward. It sees inward. When the forehead rests on something solid, the mind often grows quiet in ways it cannot when it is floating free. Let the contact with the bolster be permission to go still.
Seated Supported Forward Fold with Eye Pillow Focus
5 min
Move into a simple crossed leg seat or hero's pose on a block or folded blankets. Lean forward over a bolster, forehead on the bolster. Eye pillow on the back of the head if desired, or simply held awareness at the third eye center.
Before we seek answers, we practice being with the question. The wisdom you are looking for is not somewhere else. It has been waiting in the silence you keep not quite allowing yourself.
Chair Supported Seated Stillness
4 min
Sit in a chair or upright on a bolster, blanket supporting the back if needed. Hands in jnana mudra or simply resting. This is a transition pose, an upright preparation for the crown. Eyes remain closed. No movement.
Begin to sense the energy rising through the spine. You are becoming a column of light and awareness. The ground is still beneath you. You are still rooted. And you are also rising.
7
Sahasrara
Crown Chakra
Surrender, unity, the dissolving of separation
17 min Total
Violet / White Element: Thought
Elevated Legs Up the Wall Viparita Karani
10 min
Folded blanket or bolster under the sacrum. Legs up the wall or resting elevated on a chair seat. Eye pillow on. Blanket over the entire body. Warmth is essential for surrender. Arms wide open. Crown of the head toward the wall, creating the full inversion of energy.
The crown chakra is not something you activate. It is something you allow. When the body is fully supported, fully warm, fully still, something in us stops performing even at being relaxed. That is the moment. Right there. That is the opening.
Final Savasana
7 min
Fully supine. Bolster beneath knees. Blanket over the entire body. Eye pillow on. Head blanket. Arms slightly away from body. Allow any final micro adjustments and then absolute stillness. If working with the Reiki master, this is the integration window for Reiki work.
You came into this room carrying everything you carry. You do not have to put it down. But you may notice, here, that you are more than what you carry. Rest in that. Rest in what you are beneath the weight of it all.

Closing the Practice

Allow 3 to 5 minutes for students to return slowly, rolling to one side, pressing up to seated. No rushing. Let silence hold the room until they are ready.

Consider a brief closing, a single bell, a word of gratitude, or simply the collective breath of a room that has been somewhere together. The integration continues long after the mat is rolled up.
