# Private Somatic Work Direction And First Client Plan

Created May 26, 2026

## Why This Direction Matters

This document gathers the main insight from the shared ChatGPT conversation titled Fast Path to Income, along with the immediate next steps for the first client who has asked about individual work.

The core realization is simple: Beyond the Bend does not need to rely only on group classes, memberships, social media, funnels, or a large video library in order to become financially supportive. The strongest and most immediate path may be a more human, high value practice built around private support, workshops, retreats, and occasional corporate work.

This direction fits Laura because her strongest gifts are presence, emotional intelligence, deep listening, teaching experience, body based wisdom, reflective insight, and the ability to help women feel steadier in themselves.

## The Bigger Business Direction

The strongest near term model is:

1. Teach three to four weekly classes.
2. Offer private somatic and reflective sessions.
3. Add occasional workshops.
4. Build toward retreats over time.
5. Explore corporate wellness as higher value work.
6. Treat the video library as a future asset, not an urgent task.

This model is more spacious than trying to become a constant online creator. It also creates a faster path to meaningful income because private sessions, workshops, retreats, and corporate work all have a higher income per hour than editing large volumes of recorded classes or trying to sell low priced digital offers.

## The Private Work Positioning

The private work should not be positioned as basic yoga instruction, life coaching, or therapy.

A clearer positioning is:

Private somatic and reflective support for women navigating burnout, grief, caregiving, identity change, divorce, transition, exhaustion, or the sense that they have lost touch with themselves.

Another possible description:

A steady, body based space for women who are ready to listen inward, soften survival patterns, and begin feeling more at home in themselves again.

The value is not only the movements or the conversation. The value is the container Laura creates through presence, care, inquiry, nervous system awareness, and lived wisdom.

It is also a natural fit for women moving through perimenopause and menopause who are navigating physical changes, emotional shifts, sleep disruption, stress, identity questions, and a changing relationship with their bodies.

The first goal is not to fix the client. The first goal is to help her feel met, safe, listened to, and gently reconnected with her own inner signals.

## How To Explain The Blend

The simplest way to describe the work is:

Private somatic and restorative support that may include gentle movement, breathwork, body awareness, reflective conversation, journaling, restorative yoga, Jin Shin Do inspired self care, and guided inner practices.

For clients who are open to deeper spiritual work, some sessions may also include shamanic inspired journeying or ritual elements. These pieces should be offered with consent and only when they feel appropriate for the person, the moment, and the container.

This keeps the offer honest without making it sound scattered. The through line is not one single modality. The through line is helping the client return to her body, her inner knowing, and a steadier relationship with herself.

## Important Boundaries

This work should be described clearly and ethically.

1. This is not therapy.
2. This is not medical treatment.
3. This is not crisis care.
4. This does not diagnose mental health or physical conditions.
5. This can support body awareness, reflection, stress relief, rest, movement, and personal integration.
6. Clients with significant trauma, acute mental health needs, or medical concerns may need support from a licensed professional alongside or instead of this work.

Laura can still offer deep, beautiful, meaningful support while staying inside a clear and honest scope.

## Consultation Structure

Keep this first conversation simple and spacious.

### 1. Welcome

Thank her for reaching out and let her know there is no pressure to have everything figured out.

Possible words:

I am really glad you reached out. I thought we could use today simply to hear what is bringing you here, what you are needing, and whether this feels like a good fit.

### 2. Ask What Brought Her Here

Questions to choose from:

1. What made you reach out for individual support right now?
2. What has been feeling hardest lately?
3. What are you hoping might feel different through this work?
4. Is there anything happening in your body that you are especially aware of?
5. Are there particular areas where you feel stuck, overwhelmed, disconnected, or tired?

### 3. Ask About Support And Safety

Questions to choose from:

1. Have you done yoga, somatic work, therapy, coaching, or body based work before?
2. Is there anything I should know about injuries, health concerns, trauma history, or movement limitations?
3. Are you currently supported by a therapist, doctor, or other practitioner?
4. Are there any types of movement, touch, language, or practices that do not feel supportive for you?

Note: If touch ever becomes part of the work, get clear consent every time. It may be wise to begin with no touch while this offer is still forming.

### 4. Explain The Work Simply

Possible words:

The way I imagine this work is as a gentle, body based and reflective space. It is not therapy, and I am not here to diagnose or treat anything. What I can offer is deep listening, somatic awareness, restorative and mindful movement, breathwork, stillness, journaling, and support for beginning to hear what your body and inner life may be asking for. Depending on what feels right for you, some sessions may also include Jin Shin Do inspired self care, guided journeying, or simple ritual practices.

Each session would be shaped around what you are bringing. Some days may include more conversation and reflection. Some may include more movement, rest, breath, or grounding. The intention is to help you feel steadier, more connected, and less alone inside what you are carrying.

### 5. Share The Practical Details

Possible words:

Because this is a newer private offering, I am beginning with founding client pricing. My current rate is $100 for a 60 minute session. If we decide to continue, we can either book one session at a time or look at a simple four session package.

### 6. Close With A Clear Next Step

Possible words:

Why do you take a little time to feel into whether this feels right, and I will do the same. If it feels like a yes for both of us, we can choose a first session time and I will send you a short intake form before we begin.
