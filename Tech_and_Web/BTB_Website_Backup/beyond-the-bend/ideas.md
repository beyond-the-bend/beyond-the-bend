# Beyond the Bend Yoga — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Beyond the Bend is Laura Harvey's yoga, meditation, and somatic movement practice in Sherwood Park, Alberta. The brand serves women in midlife navigating burnout, transitions, and disconnection. The brand guide specifies: Sage Green (#A8B5A2), Soft Blush (#E8C4C4), Warm Sand (#E6D5C7), Deep Sage (#6B8063), Muted Rosewood (#B06568), Dark Olive (#3F4A3C). Fonts: Playfair Display (headings) + Lato (body). Voice: calm, grounded, warm, quietly powerful.

## Idea A: "The Wabi-Sabi Retreat"

**Design Movement:** Japanese Wabi-Sabi meets Scandinavian Hygge — the beauty of imperfection, natural materials, and quiet presence.

**Core Principles:**
- Asymmetric, off-center compositions that feel organic rather than engineered
- Generous negative space as a design element, not an absence
- Warmth through texture: linen-like backgrounds, subtle grain overlays
- Content hierarchy driven by weight and position, not color

**Color Philosophy:** The palette is used with restraint. Warm Sand (#E6D5C7) dominates as the primary background, creating a parchment-like warmth. Deep Sage (#6B8063) appears only for key calls to action and structural accents. Rosewood (#B06568) is used once per page for a single emotional highlight. The overall effect is quiet and unhurried.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial grid. Hero sections are split — image occupies 60% left, text floats right at 40% with generous padding. Section transitions use diagonal or organic cuts rather than straight horizontal lines. Content blocks alternate between full-bleed and contained.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rules (1px, sage-colored) used as section dividers instead of background color changes
- Large italic Playfair Display pull-quotes that break the grid intentionally
- Subtle grain/noise texture overlay on all backgrounds (3-5% opacity)

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions are slow and deliberate. Hover states fade in over 400ms. Scroll-triggered reveals use gentle upward drift. Nothing snaps or jumps.

**Animation:** Fade-up on scroll (opacity 0→1, translateY 30px→0, 800ms ease-out). Hero image has a slow 14s Ken Burns zoom. No bouncing, no spring physics.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display Italic at 4-5rem for hero headlines. Lato 300 weight for body at 1.1rem/1.8 line-height. Small caps uppercase Lato at 0.7rem/0.25em letter-spacing for eyebrow labels.

## Idea B: "The Forest Sanctuary"

**Design Movement:** Organic Modernism — the intersection of natural forms and clean contemporary structure.

**Core Principles:**
- Full-bleed photography as the primary design element
- Text as a quiet companion to imagery, never competing
- Vertical rhythm established through consistent spacing units
- Soft, rounded forms in UI elements (cards, buttons) echoing natural shapes

**Color Philosophy:** Deep Sage (#6B8063) and Dark Olive (#3F4A3C) form a rich forest-toned foundation. Light Cream (#F7F4EF) provides breathing room. Blush (#E8C4C4) is used as a warm accent against the green tones, creating a sense of dawn light filtering through trees.

**Layout Paradigm:** Stacked full-width sections with alternating image-left / text-right compositions. Cards use a masonry-style arrangement for the library section. Navigation is minimal — wordmark left, three key links right.

**Signature Elements:**
- Leaf-shaped SVG decorative elements at section transitions
- Soft vignette overlays on hero images creating a "looking through a window" effect
- Cards with a subtle inner shadow suggesting depth and tactility

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions feel like breathing. Hover states expand slightly (scale 1.02). Page transitions are a soft cross-fade.

**Animation:** Cards lift on hover (translateY -6px, shadow deepens). Section reveals are staggered — each element in a group enters 100ms after the previous.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display 400 weight for headlines. Cormorant Garamond Italic for subheadings and pull-quotes. Lato 300 for body text.

## Idea C: "The Still Water"

**Design Movement:** Contemplative Minimalism — inspired by meditation retreat aesthetics and Japanese ink wash painting.

**Core Principles:**
- Maximum restraint: every element must earn its place
- Horizontal space is sacred — wide margins, narrow content columns
- Photography is used sparingly but powerfully, always full-bleed
- Typography carries the emotional weight of the design

**Color Philosophy:** Near-monochromatic. The background is #F7F4EF (warm cream). Text is #3F4A3C (dark olive). The only color accent is a single sage green (#A8B5A2) used for lines, borders, and one button per section. This restraint creates a meditative, focused reading experience.

**Layout Paradigm:** Single-column editorial layout with very wide margins (20% each side on desktop). Sections are separated by generous whitespace (200px) rather than dividers. Images break out of the column to full-width occasionally, creating visual punctuation.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin vertical lines (1px sage) used as decorative elements in the left margin
- Large numerals (01, 02, 03) in a very light weight as section markers
- Pull-quotes set in large Playfair Display italic that span the full content width

**Interaction Philosophy:** Nothing moves unless it must. Hover states are a simple color shift. The experience rewards slow, careful reading.

**Animation:** Minimal. Only scroll-triggered opacity fades (no movement). Duration 600ms.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display at varying sizes (3rem to 6rem) for headlines. Lato 300 at 1.05rem for body. Small caps for navigation and labels.

## Selected Approach: Idea A — "The Wabi-Sabi Retreat"

This approach best matches Laura's brand: organic, warm, asymmetric, and unhurried. The linen-textured backgrounds, editorial typography, and slow animations create the feeling of a retreat space rather than a website. The restraint in color use ensures the brand palette reads as intentional and sophisticated rather than decorative.
