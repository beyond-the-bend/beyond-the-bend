# The Fire Journey: Spoken Word Poem (Delivery Script)
*Graduation Performance Guide*

***

### How to use this script:
*   **[pause]** = Take a deep, conscious breath. Let the last line settle with the audience.
*   **[beat]** = A short, rhythmic hesitation to build momentum.
*   **Bold words** = Lean in slightly. Give these words more vocal weight and warmth.

***

<div style="font-size: 32px; line-height: 1.8; font-family: sans-serif; padding: 10px;">

For a long time,<br>
the world taught us a <strong>cold script</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
It told us that life happens <strong>to me</strong>.<br>
Shivering in the dark,<br>
a victim to the wind and the rain.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
Then we shifted,<br>
thinking it happens <strong>because of me</strong>.<br>
Heavy with the weight of <strong>self blame</strong>,<br>
suffocating our own light.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Or we looked out at the ash<br>
and decided that life is just spiteful,<br>
that the whole thing just <strong>sucks</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
But we came to this fire journey to <strong>rewrite the context</strong>.<br>
We began to feel life moving <strong>through me</strong>,<br>
like a warm draft feeding the coals.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
We watched it evolve into happening <strong>for me</strong>.<br>
Every obstacle,<br>
<strong>fuel</strong> for the fire.<br>
Every cold wind,<br>
the <strong>oxygen</strong> that makes the flames rise.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Until we touched the quiet truth<br>
that life is happening <strong>as me</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
You are <strong>not separate</strong> from the fire.<br>
You are the <strong>flame itself</strong>.<br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
But nothing makes sense <strong>without context</strong>.<br>
That old lens we wore,<br>
built in childhood,<br>
our <strong>deserving context</strong>,<br>
telling us we were <strong>not enough</strong>,<br>
trying to smother our warmth with the fear of <strong>abandonment</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
But we found the <strong>cracks</strong> in the lens.<br>
And the cracks are where the <strong>air gets in</strong>,<br>
where the fire learns to breathe.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
We <strong>flipped</strong> the script.<br>
Not enough became <strong>capable</strong>.<br>
Abandonment became <strong>safety</strong>.<br>
Disappointment became <strong>enough</strong>.<br>
Chaos became <strong>challenge</strong>.<br>
Conflict became <strong>communication</strong>.<br>
Unworthiness became <strong>courage</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
We stepped out of the <strong>automatic reactions</strong>.<br>
We broke the <strong>resistance cycle</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
If we are not feeling neutral,<br>
we are in <strong>resistance</strong>,<br>
fighting the flame.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
So we choose the path of growth, joy, cause, choice, and <strong>surrender</strong>.<br>
Surrender to the <strong>story</strong>.<br>
Surrender to the <strong>truth</strong>.<br>
That is how the old beliefs <strong>burn away</strong>.<br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
But as the light grows,<br>
we must also look at the <strong>shadows</strong>.<br>
The voices that whisper in the corners,<br>
the ones that try to keep us <strong>small</strong>.<br>
<br>
Our <strong>saboteurs</strong>.<br>
The <strong>Judge</strong>, finding fault, throwing water on your spark.<br>
The <strong>Pleaser</strong>, giving your heat away.<br>
The Avoider, the Controller, the Victim.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
They are <strong>not</strong> the truth of who you are.<br>
They are just the <strong>survival suits</strong> of a child.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
<strong>Quiet</strong> them.<br>
And step into your <strong>Sage</strong>.<br>
The wise, quiet center of the flame.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
And ask the only question that matters:
<strong>What is my stand?</strong><br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Because you have to know where you stand <strong>to lead</strong>.<br>
You have to know your own heat <strong>to warm others</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
We spent a weekend in the <strong>territory of fear</strong>.<br>
We watched how we lean in<br>
and how we lean out of the fire.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
We saw fear as <strong>False Evidence Appearing Real</strong>.<br>
Cold smoke rising in our heads.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
But we chose to <strong>play to win</strong>.<br>
Not playing <strong>not to lose</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
We chose to <strong>risk</strong>.<br>
To <strong>share</strong>.<br>
To <strong>grow</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
We realized <strong>courage is not confidence</strong>.<br>
You do not need confidence to start.<br>
Enough courage over time creates the <strong>fire of confidence</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
And <strong>vulnerability</strong> is the only door.<br>
Yes, you might get burned.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
But <strong>what we focus on expands</strong>,<br>
and what we resist persists.<br>
So we focus on our <strong>growth</strong>.<br>
We focus on <strong>Face Everything And Rise</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
We step <strong>directly</strong> into the heat.<br>
There is <strong>nothing</strong> in your life you are afraid to meet.<br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
And we build our leadership on <strong>four steady stones</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
First: <strong>Integrity</strong>.<br>
The way you were <strong>designed</strong> to burn.<br>
Not a perfect flame, but a <strong>steady</strong> one.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
And when the spark misses, remember:<br>
There is <strong>no such thing as mistakes</strong>.<br>
Only a <strong>mis strike</strong> of the match.<br>
Only a <strong>retake</strong>.<br>
A chance to strike once more,<br>
to find your alignment in the dark.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Second: <strong>Authenticity</strong>.<br>
Your own <strong>unique color</strong> of flame.<br>
Not copying the glow of others,<br>
but feeding your own hearth.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
Finding your truth in the <strong>quiet reflection</strong> of the embers,<br>
and naming it <strong>out loud</strong>, without hiding.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Third: <strong>Source and Cause</strong>.<br>
Knowing <strong>you</strong> are the one holding the match.<br>
You are the friction that creates the heat.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
When another's behavior sparks tension in you, you do not <strong>blame</strong>.<br>
You say:<br>
<em>This is the behavior I notice, and this is the interpretation I am bringing.</em><br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
You **own** the smoke you bring to the room.<br>
Knowing the wind may blow, but you control your own flame:<br>
How you <strong>think</strong>.<br>
How you <strong>feel</strong>.<br>
How you <strong>act</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
All events are <strong>neutral</strong>,<br>
just dry wood waiting for a meaning.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
So you teach others where your <strong>fire line</strong> is.<br>
You set your boundaries.<br>
You take action.<br>
You <strong>just do it</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Fourth: <strong>Being Bigger Than Me</strong>.<br>
Letting your individual flame reach <strong>outward</strong>.<br>
Not just warming your own hands,<br>
but joining a <strong>larger hearth</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
Standing with those who choose to burn <strong>bright</strong>,<br>
until we are a <strong>bonfire</strong> of connection,<br>
raising the heat for the <strong>whole room</strong>.<br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
We proved it.<br>
We showed the world what happens when we <strong>work together</strong>.<br>
That night at <strong>Rosario's</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
On that stage, in that crowded room,<br>
building a <strong>sanctuary of sound</strong>.<br>
Our final project together.<br>
Our hearts <strong>on fire</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
We created the <strong>Purpose Project</strong>, rising in rhythm.<br>
We chose to <strong>know our neighbor</strong>.<br>
We chose connection, friendship, and community.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
We stood <strong>side by side</strong>, singing together,<br>
live music bringing <strong>real impact</strong>,<br>
proving that when our individual sparks gather,<br>
we are <strong>stronger together</strong>.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
That night at Rosario's was the proof:<br>
When we <strong>show up</strong>,<br>
the world gets <strong>warmer</strong>.<br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
So we choose how we want to live our lives,<br>
the <strong>five states of consciousness</strong> Gandhi mapped for the soul.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
Will we live in **Apathy**, cold ash, choosing not to care?<br>
Will we live **Passive**, a smothered spark, hiding from the light?<br>
Will we live in **Self Righteousness**, a wild fire of judgment, burning others to feel warm?<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
Or will we live in **Courageous Action**,<br>
stepping up,<br>
acting **despite** the fear?<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
And finally, will we live in **Nonviolence**?<br>
The highest state,<br>
the **cleanest** burn,<br>
the ultimate way to live,<br>
found only through the **cooling waters of forgiveness**.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Taking accountability for the reactions we brought to the room.<br>
Forgiving the stories we made up.<br>
Choosing to burn with **love** instead of fear.<br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
We make a **conscious choice** about the energy we generate.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
Will you be a **taker**?<br>
A **drainer**?<br>
An energy vampire, sucking the oxygen from the room,<br>
leaving nothing but cold soot?<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Or will you be a **giver**?<br>
A **generator**?<br>
A **radiant hearth**,<br>
leaving everyone you touch **expanded**, **bright**, and **alive**?<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
**Enroll** in your own life.<br>
Choose to show up **every single day**.<br>
Engage others.<br>
Subscribe them.<br>
**Inspire** them to subscribe to their own light.<br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Listen to your **intuition**.<br>
It is **somatically felt**.<br>
Grounded.<br>
Centered.<br>
Like hot coals resting in the **core** of your body.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
Choose to be **Big Me** all of the time,<br>
not just **some** of the time.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Step into the **Be**, **Do**, and **Have** of your flame:<br>
**Be:** grateful, radiant, courageous, connected, beautiful.<br>
**Do:** surrender, take action, remain vulnerable.<br>
**Have:** fun, gratitude, and courage.<br>
<br>
<hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc; margin: 40px 0;">
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Because **participation** creates value.<br>
**Attitude** creates experience.<br>
**Intention and action** create results.<br>
<br>
<strong>[beat]</strong><br>
<br>
This is the **fire journey**.<br>
You are the **leader of your life**.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Hold the stone in your hand.<br>
Feel its weight.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
Take a breath.<br>
<br>
<strong>[pause]</strong><br>
<br>
And **rise**.<br>
<br>
