## Caption

Most of us know what it feels like to live from the neck up.

Thinking, planning, anticipating, remembering, deciding, and trying to hold the whole shape of life together from the mind.

The Sacred Journey: From Crown to Root is an evening for gently descending out of that constant mental holding and back into the steadier wisdom of the body.

We will move through restorative yoga, somatic practice, Reiki and energy work, journaling, tea, fruit, and quiet. The chakra system will be used as a gentle map, not as something you need to understand before you come, and not as something you need to fix.

This is meant to be a safe, carefully held space where your body does not have to perform before it can receive care.

You do not need to arrive certain, spiritual, flexible, or prepared. You only need a willingness to slow down and be supported.
