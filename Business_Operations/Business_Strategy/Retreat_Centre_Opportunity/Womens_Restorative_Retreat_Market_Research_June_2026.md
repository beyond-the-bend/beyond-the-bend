# Womens Restorative Retreat Market Research

Created June 17, 2026

## Research Scope

This pass looked at women’s restorative retreats, women’s wellness weekends, burnout restoration retreats, yoga retreats, and related mini retreat events in the Edmonton, Sherwood Park, Ardrossan, Leduc County, and nearby Alberta market.

The clearest source for local comparable pricing was Eventbrite, because several relevant retreats are actively listed there.

## Biggest Finding

There is already a visible local market for women’s restorative and wellness retreat experiences near Edmonton and Sherwood Park.

The strongest comparable is:

```text
EXHALE, A WOMENS RESTORATIVE RETREAT
Rising Moon Studio, Sherwood Park
Friday, October 2 to Sunday, October 4
Observed price from Eventbrite:
From $1,011.51
```

This matters because it is almost exactly the type of retreat being considered: a women’s restorative weekend with accommodation, restorative yoga, yoga nidra, sound bath, forest bathing, reflective practices, Nordic hot and cold baths, sauna, indoor and outdoor yoga spaces, and shared accommodations.

This suggests that a well presented women’s restorative weekend in the Edmonton area can potentially support pricing around $1,000 per person when accommodation and a full retreat experience are included.

## Pricing Ladder Suggested By The Market

Based on the examples above, a realistic pricing ladder for Laura’s future women’s restorative retreat could look like this:

```text
Short evening circle or sacred rest event:
$45 to $75

Mini retreat, two to three hours:
$65 to $125

Full day restorative retreat:
$175 to $275

Premium full day restorative retreat with food, land, horses, and multiple experiences:
$250 to $350

Weekend retreat with shared accommodation:
$650 to $1,050

Premium weekend retreat with strong amenities, beautiful lodging, horses, hot tub, sauna, food, and polished facilitation:
$950 to $1,400
```

## What This Means For The Retreat Property

The earlier retreat estimate of:

```text
10 participants at $650 equals $6,500 gross

12 participants at $750 equals $9,000 gross

14 participants at $850 equals $11,900 gross

20 participants at $850 equals $17,000 gross

25 participants at $850 equals $21,250 gross
```

is not unreasonable.

In fact, the EXHALE retreat at Rising Moon suggests that a local women’s restorative weekend with lodging and strong amenities can potentially support $1,000 plus per person.

If the retreat property could sleep 20 to 25 people and the experience was polished, a premium women’s restorative weekend could eventually look like:

```text
16 guests at $950 equals $15,200 gross

20 guests at $950 equals $19,000 gross

20 guests at $1,100 equals $22,000 gross

25 guests at $1,100 equals $27,500 gross
```

These are gross numbers before food, cleaning, facilitator pay, supplies, insurance, payment processing, laundry, marketing, and owner split.

## Market Takeaway

The local market supports the idea that women will pay meaningful money for restorative retreat experiences when the offer includes:

```text
A beautiful setting
Clear emotional promise
Lodging or extended time
Multiple forms of rest
Food or hospitality
Special amenities
Skilled facilitation
Community with other women
Permission to opt in and out of activities
```

For a first pilot retreat, the safest price may be:

```text
$650 to $850 per person
```

For planning purposes, $1,000 per person is a strong working number. This is based primarily on the Rising Moon comparison. Laura teaches at Rising Moon and knows the space. If this property is larger, more beautiful, more private, and eventually includes a hot tub and sauna, then a $1,000 per person weekend retreat price is reasonable as a planning anchor.

For a more polished version with strong accommodations, food, land, horses, hot tub or sauna, and a full weekend container, the market appears to support:

```text
$950 to $1,200 per person
```

If the retreat becomes very strong and the guest experience is excellent, a premium range of:

```text
$1,200 to $1,400 per person
```

could be possible later.
