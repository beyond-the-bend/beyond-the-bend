Purpose Of This Document

This is a working outline for the planning conversation with Zorana. It is not final public copy.

The goal is to make Laura's part of the workshop feel grounded, meaningful, organized, and special. The workshop is not simply a long restorative yoga class. It is a three hour journey through the seven chakras, with restorative yoga, breathwork, meditation, reflection, and Reiki woven together.

Big Picture Intention

The workshop introduces the seven chakras as a map of the body, the inner life, and the human journey from grounding to connection.

The tone should be:

  1. Grounded.
  2. Spacious.
  3. Reflective.
  4. Accessible.
  5. Spiritual without being vague.
  6. Restorative without feeling passive.
  7. Deep, but not emotionally overwhelming.

The central invitation:

We are not trying to fix or force the chakras into balance. We are listening through the body, noticing where there is flow, where there is holding, and where each center may be asking for care.

Shared Structure With Zorana

The two facilitators can work in tandem rather than taking separate turns.

Possible rhythm for each chakra:

  1. Laura introduces the chakra in simple, embodied language.
  2. Laura leads students into the restorative shape or breath practice.
  3. Zorana offers Reiki while students are held in the posture.
  4. Laura offers short spoken cues, imagery, or silence.
  5. Laura closes the chakra with one sentence of integration or one reflective question.
  6. Zorana may add a brief energetic insight or closing phrase if that feels natural.

This keeps the workshop collaborative, but not crowded.

Suggested Timing

Total time: Three hours.

  1. Arrival and opening: Fifteen minutes.
  2. Root chakra: Eighteen minutes.
  3. Sacral chakra: Eighteen minutes.
  4. Solar plexus chakra: Eighteen minutes.
  5. Heart chakra: Twenty minutes.
  6. Short pause and water: Eight minutes.
  7. Throat chakra: Eighteen minutes.
  8. Third eye chakra: Eighteen minutes.
  9. Crown chakra: Twenty minutes.
  10. Final integration, journaling, and closing: Twenty five minutes.

This gives enough room for the work to breathe and gives Zorana time to offer Reiki without making each chakra feel rushed.

Opening, Fifteen Minutes

Laura's Role

Welcome everyone into the space. Name that this is an experiential journey, not a lecture. Invite students to listen to their own bodies and take care of themselves.

Tonight we are going to move through the seven chakras as a map of the body and the inner life. We are not here to perfect anything or force anything open. We are here to listen, to rest, to notice, and to allow the body to show us where there may be steadiness, tenderness, holding, or quiet wisdom.

Opening Practice

  1. Comfortable seated position or reclined constructive rest.
  2. One hand on the lower belly, one hand on the heart.
  3. Three slow breaths.
  4. Invite students to notice the support beneath them.
  5. Let Zorana introduce the Reiki element and how she will be moving through the room.

Opening Question

What am I carrying into this room, and what would I like to set down for the next three hours?

1

Root Chakra, Muladhara

Theme: Safety, belonging, steadiness, the right to be here.
Element: Earth.
Body focus: Feet, legs, pelvis, base of spine, weight.

Laura's Restorative Practice

Rooted constructive rest with feet on the floor.

This is stronger than placing a bolster under the knees because the soles of the feet stay connected to the ground. For the root chakra, that contact matters. Students should be able to feel the floor through the feet, the weight of the pelvis, and the support of the earth underneath them. A folded blanket between the knees with a strap around the thighs adds containment, so the legs can stay connected without effort.

Set up:

  1. Students lie on their backs with knees bent.
  2. Feet are placed on the floor, about hip width apart.
  3. Heels are close enough to the body that the legs can rest without gripping.
  4. A folded blanket can go under the head.
  5. A blanket can cover the body for warmth.
  6. Optional eye pillow.
  7. Place a folded blanket between the knees.
  8. Add a strap loosely around the thighs so the legs feel held and contained without muscular effort.
  9. Invite students to feel the four corners of each foot, the weight of the pelvis, and the back body resting into the mat.

Optional variation: If someone cannot comfortably keep the feet on the floor, they can place calves on a chair instead, but the main teaching cue should stay with the felt sense of grounding through the lower body.

Breath Practice

Weighted exhale breath.

Breathe in and feel the body gently expand. Breathe out and let the soles of the feet become heavier. Feel the floor meeting you through the feet, the pelvis, and the back body. Nothing to hold. Nothing to prove. Just the simple fact of being here.

Meditation Cue

The root asks: Do I feel supported enough to soften?

Reiki Weaving

This is a good place for Zorana to offer grounding Reiki, beginning at the feet or lower body if that is her practice. Laura can hold silence while Zorana works, then close with a short phrase.

Closing phrase: May the ground beneath you remind you that you are allowed to be here.

2

Sacral Chakra, Svadhisthana

Theme: Feeling, flow, pleasure, creativity, emotional permission.
Element: Water.
Body focus: Hips, pelvis, lower belly, fluid body.

Laura's Restorative Practice

Supported reclined butterfly.

Set up:

  1. Bolster lengthwise under the spine.
  2. Soles of feet together.
  3. Knees supported by blocks or blankets.
  4. Blanket over pelvis and belly.
  5. Encourage full support under the thighs so there is no gripping.

Breath Practice

Pelvic bowl breath.

Imagine the pelvis as a bowl of water. With each inhale, the water gently fills. With each exhale, the surface softens. There is nothing to force open here. We are practicing the possibility of flow.

Meditation Cue

The sacral asks: What feelings have I been bracing against, and what might soften if I stopped resisting them?

Reiki Weaving

Zorana may work with the lower abdomen, hips, or sacral field while Laura offers silence. This chakra may be tender for some people, so the language should stay gentle and choice based.

Closing phrase: May what has been held too tightly begin to find its own movement.

3

Solar Plexus Chakra, Manipura

Theme: Will, identity, boundaries, confidence, personal authority.
Element: Fire.
Body focus: Belly, ribs, diaphragm, center line.

Laura's Restorative Practice

Supported prone rest with belly support.

Set up:

  1. Students lie face down.
  2. Bolster or folded blankets under abdomen and lower ribs.
  3. Forehead rests on hands or turned to one side.
  4. Blanket over back if desired.
  5. Encourage softness in the belly and jaw.

Breath Practice

Steady center breath.

Let the breath move into the place beneath the ribs. Not a forceful breath, not a fiery push, just a steady warmth at the center of you.

Meditation Cue

The solar plexus asks: Where have I given away my power, and where am I ready to stand quietly within myself again?

Reiki Weaving

This is a strong place for Reiki around the solar plexus, the diaphragm, or the back body. Laura can keep the cueing minimal so students feel held rather than pushed.

Closing phrase: May your power return to you without force, without apology, and without needing to harden.

4

Heart Chakra, Anahata

Theme: Love, grief, tenderness, connection, receiving.
Element: Air.
Body focus: Chest, upper back, lungs, arms, hands.

Laura's Restorative Practice

Supported heart opener over bolster.

Set up:

  1. Bolster under the spine or across the shoulder blades depending on what feels most accessible.
  2. Blanket under head.
  3. Arms supported by blankets in a cactus shape or resting wide.
  4. Legs extended or knees supported.
  5. Make sure the backbend is gentle, not dramatic.

Breath Practice

Three part receiving breath.

Breathe into the back of the heart. Breathe into the sides of the ribs. Breathe into the front of the chest. Let the breath arrive from all directions, as if support could reach you from more than one place.

Meditation Cue

The heart asks: What have I protected, and what am I ready to let be held?

Reiki Weaving

This may be one of the strongest Reiki moments. Zorana may work around the heart field while Laura gives more silence. This is also a good place for soft music or a longer quiet pause.

Closing phrase: May the heart remember that opening and protection can both be forms of wisdom.

Short Pause, Eight Minutes

Purpose: Let students return slowly, drink water, use the washroom, and stay inward.

We will take a few quiet minutes here. Try to stay close to the practice. Move slowly, drink some water, and notice what is present before we continue upward.

5

Throat Chakra, Vishuddha

Theme: Voice, truth, expression, listening.
Element: Sound.
Body focus: Throat, jaw, neck, mouth, ears.

Laura's Restorative Practice

Supported fish or supported reclined throat opener.

Set up:

  1. Bolster lengthwise under spine.
  2. Blanket roll under neck if needed.
  3. Head supported enough that there is no strain.
  4. Arms open or hands resting on lower ribs.
  5. Keep the throat opening gentle.

Breath Practice

Humming exhale.

Inhale through the nose. As you exhale, offer a soft hum, just enough to feel vibration in the throat, lips, jaw, or chest. This is not performance. This is sound as medicine for the inner body.

Meditation Cue

The throat asks: What truth has been waiting for a safe enough space to be heard?

Reiki Weaving

Zorana may work around the throat, shoulders, jaw, or upper chest. Laura can offer two or three rounds of humming, then silence.

Closing phrase: May your voice return first as vibration, then as truth, then as a way of belonging to yourself.

6

Third Eye Chakra, Ajna

Theme: Intuition, inner seeing, imagination, clarity.
Element: Light.
Body focus: Brow, eyes, forehead, inner gaze.

Laura's Restorative Practice

Supported forward fold with forehead contact.

Set up:

  1. Seated with legs crossed, kneeling, or wide legged depending on comfort.
  2. Bolster or stacked blocks in front.
  3. Forehead rests on support.
  4. Arms relaxed.
  5. The key is gentle pressure at the forehead.

Breath Practice

Quiet counting breath.

Inhale for a slow count of four. Exhale for a slow count of six. Let the longer exhale quiet the surface of the mind so the deeper seeing has room to come forward.

Meditation Cue

The third eye asks: What do I know when I stop arguing with my own knowing?

Reiki Weaving

Zorana may offer Reiki around the head, brow, or crown preparation. Laura can guide a brief visualization of indigo light or simply invite inner listening.

Closing phrase: May you trust the quiet knowing that does not need to shout.

7

Crown Chakra, Sahasrara

Theme: Surrender, meaning, connection, unity, being held by something larger.
Element: Thought or spirit.
Body focus: Crown of head, vertical axis, whole body.

Laura's Restorative Practice

Final supported savasana or elevated legs on chair.

Set up:

  1. Students lie on their backs.
  2. Bolster under knees or legs on chairs.
  3. Blanket over body.
  4. Eye pillow.
  5. Head supported.
  6. Full warmth and stillness.

Breath Practice

No technique, just natural breath.

Let the breath breathe itself. Let the body be breathed. Nothing to manage now. Nothing to improve. Just the quiet possibility of being held.

Meditation Cue

The crown asks: What supports me beyond my own effort?

Reiki Weaving

This is the strongest integration window for Zorana. Laura can hold a long silence while Zorana offers Reiki. This should feel like the culmination of the journey, not just the final pose.

Closing phrase: May you remember that you do not have to carry your life alone.

Final Integration, Twenty Five Minutes

Return

  1. Deepen breath.
  2. Feel the support beneath the body.
  3. Small movement in fingers and toes.
  4. Roll to one side.
  5. Pause before coming up.
  6. Return to seated.

Journaling

Offer three prompts and let students choose one.

  1. Which chakra felt most alive or open tonight?
  2. Which chakra felt most tender, quiet, blocked, or in need of care?
  3. What is one gentle action I can take this week to support that part of myself?

Closing Circle

Students may share one word, or sharing can be optional.

Thank you for taking this journey through the body with us. You may notice things continuing to move or settle after tonight. Be gentle with yourself. Drink water, rest if you can, and let the practice keep unfolding without needing to understand it all right away.
