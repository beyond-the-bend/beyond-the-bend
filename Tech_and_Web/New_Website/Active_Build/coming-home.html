The Eight Week Arc

Module 01

Recognizing Where You Are

Learning to see and name depletion without judgment. Understanding your current nervous system state and the patterns that brought you here.

The Practice
  • Awareness Body Scan
  • Grounding Foundations
  • Tracing Depletion
  • Energy Pattern Tracking
Module 02

Understanding Your Nervous System

The foundation of why you feel the way you do. Learning the language of your nervous system and how it has been trying to protect you.

The Practice
  • Vagal Tone Breathing
  • Parasympathetic Activation
  • Trigger Mapping
  • Nervous System Language
Module 03

Creating Safety in Your Body

Establishing the felt sense of safety that allows your nervous system to shift from survival to restoration.

The Practice
  • Safety Anchors
  • Stability Flows
  • Tension Mapping
  • Moments of Ease
Module 04

Befriending Your Breath

Deepening your relationship with breath as a bridge between stress and calm, conscious and unconscious.

The Practice
  • Extended Breathwork
  • Breath Led Movement
  • Rhythm Awareness
  • Stress Response Shifts
Module 05

Releasing What No Longer Serves

Learning to gently release stored tension and the protective mechanisms that have become burdens.

The Practice
  • Emotional Clearing
  • Hip & Spine Release
  • Legacy Unloading
  • Capacity Cultivation
Module 06

Cultivating Restoration

Moving from not stressed to genuinely nourished. Building practices that fill your well rather than just stopping the drain.

The Practice
  • Restorative Visuals
  • Long Sequence Yin
  • Self Compassion
  • Energy Filling
Module 07

Awakening Vitality

The natural emergence of aliveness when your nervous system feels safe and your body feels home.

The Practice
  • Energy Cultivation
  • Vitality Flow
  • Sustainable Radiance
  • Celebrating Joy
Module 08

Living From Home

Creating a sustainable daily practice and life rhythms that support ongoing aliveness and connection.

The Practice
  • Morning Rituals
  • Integration Rhythms
  • Sustainable Planning
  • Ongoing Connection
