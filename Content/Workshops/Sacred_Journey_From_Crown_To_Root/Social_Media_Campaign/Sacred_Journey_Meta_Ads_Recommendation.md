# Sacred Journey Meta Ads Recommendation

Created June 29, 2026

## Recommendation

Put $180 to $225 CAD toward Meta ads for The Sacred Journey, with $195 CAD as the recommended working budget.

Do not spend more than $250 CAD unless registrations from ads are clearly coming in. Do not spend more than $300 CAD for this single workshop unless the goal shifts from filling seats to broader brand awareness.

## Why The Budget Should Stay Lean

The workshop has a modest ticket price, and only a limited number of spaces remain. That means a large ad budget could quickly become out of proportion to the revenue available from the remaining registrations.

The purpose of ads here is not to replace the organic campaign. The purpose is to add local reach, remind warm followers, and make sure the invitation is seen by people in Sherwood Park and nearby Edmonton who may not already receive the newsletter.

## Current Ad Cost Context

A current Shopify benchmark published in November 2025 reports average Facebook ad costs of about $0.87 USD per click and $16.06 USD per one thousand impressions. It also notes that campaign objective, audience, placement, ad quality, timing, and frequency all affect actual cost.

The same source notes that using both Facebook and Instagram placements can reduce average cost per result, and that frequency should be watched because repeated exposure to the same small audience can weaken results.

For this local workshop, expect costs to vary. A small local audience may cost more per click than a broad national campaign, but the relevance can still be worth it if the people reached are close enough to attend.

## Audience Setup

Use Meta Ads Manager rather than only boosting a post.

Ad location:

1. Sherwood Park.

2. Edmonton and nearby communities within reasonable driving distance.

Age:

1. Start with age 40 to 70.

2. If Meta suggests a broader audience, keep the location tight.

Gender:

1. The organic brand voice speaks primarily to women.

2. Ads can be centered on women for budget efficiency, but public copy should not say the workshop is only for women.

Interests to test:

1. Yoga.

2. Restorative yoga.

3. Meditation.

4. Reiki.

5. Mindfulness.

6. Wellness.

7. Rising Moon Studio followers or engagement if available.

Warm audiences:

1. People who engage with the Facebook Page.

2. People who engage with Instagram.

3. Website visitors if the Meta pixel is active.

4. Newsletter list custom audience only if it is already set up and permission is clear.

## Placements

Use Advantage placements if possible so Meta can distribute across Facebook and Instagram.

Make sure the creative works in:

1. Facebook feed.

2. Instagram feed.

3. Facebook Stories.

4. Instagram Stories.

If the square image crops poorly in Stories, use a simple vertical story graphic with the same title, date, and registration invitation.

## Campaign Objective

Use Traffic if the registration page does not have reliable conversion tracking.

Use Leads or Sales only if GoHighLevel and Meta tracking are confirmed to pass registration actions back into Ads Manager.

For this workshop, Traffic optimized for landing page views is the safest simple setup.

## Ad Copy Rules

1. Do not imply the viewer personally has anxiety, burnout, trauma, pain, or a problem.

2. Use neutral invitation language.

3. Do not promise healing or emotional release.

4. Do not use exact remaining space counts.

5. Do not use dashes.

6. Do not make the ad feel more mystical or dramatic than the actual room will feel.

## What To Watch

Check ads every two days.

Look at:

1. Amount spent.

2. Reach.

3. Link clicks.

4. Landing page views.

5. Cost per landing page view.

6. Comments.

7. Registrations since the ad began.

8. Frequency.

If frequency rises above three and registrations are not moving, refresh the copy or reduce spend.

If people are clicking but not registering, check the registration page on a phone.

If one ad clearly gets clicks at a lower cost, move the remaining budget toward that ad.

## Practical Budget Guardrails

1. If one registration comes from ads, keep the campaign running at the planned budget.

2. If two or more registrations come from ads before July 21, allow the total budget to rise to $225 CAD.

3. If no registrations come from ads by July 15, reduce spend to $5 CAD per day and rely more heavily on organic posts, stories, newsletter mentions, and direct local sharing.

4. If the workshop is nearly full, pause the ads immediately and use organic stories only.
