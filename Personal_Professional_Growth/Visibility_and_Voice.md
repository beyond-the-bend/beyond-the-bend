# Visibility and Voice

It sounds like that workshop was a huge turning point for you. Taking that realization home, that you have a hard time being visible, is a big step in moving forward. 

There is a beautiful irony in your work. Your primary goal with Beyond the Bend is to make the "invisible" woman feel seen, heard, and safe. Now, you are discovering that you have been using your own teaching space as a way to remain invisible yourself.

### Reflections on the Workshop

You mentioned that you shrink when you are in front of a group. In your yoga classes, you feel at home because everyone is concentrating on their own practice. You feel you can be invisible while teaching them. 

The shift to public speaking removes that shield. When you speak, the attention is solely on you. That spotlight can feel exposing, like your light might be too much, or that being small is safer. 

### Moving Forward

You are already doing the work. The Integrity program is teaching you more about yourself. You recognize that you cannot keep hiding your light. 

We can use this space to work through these layers. Every time you feel that pull to shrink, we can document it here and look for ways to expand that boundary. 

You are teaching women to come home to themselves. This is you coming home to your own voice.
