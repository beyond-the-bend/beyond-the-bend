Our Shared Fire Journey

A Gift from My Heart to Yours
How to use this script:
  • [PAUSE] = Take a deep, conscious breath. Let the last line settle with the audience.
  • [BEAT] = A short, rhythmic hesitation to build momentum.
  • Bold words = Lean in slightly. Give these words more vocal weight and warmth.
For a long time,
the world taught us a cold script.
[pause]
It told us that life happens to me.
Shivering in the dark,
a victim to the wind and the rain.
[beat]
Then we shifted,
thinking it happens because of me.
Heavy with the weight of self blame,
suffocating our own light.
[pause]
Or we looked out at the ash
and decided that life is just spiteful,
that the whole thing just sucks.
[beat]
But we came to this fire journey to rewrite the context.
We began to feel life moving through me,
like a warm draft feeding the coals.
[beat]
We watched it evolve into happening for me.
Every obstacle,
fuel for the fire.
Every cold wind,
the oxygen that makes the flames rise.
[pause]
Until we touched the quiet truth
that life is happening as me.
[pause]
You are not separate from the fire.
You are the flame itself.
[pause]
But nothing makes sense without context.
That old lens we wore,
built in childhood,
our deserving context,
telling us we were not enough,
trying to smother our warmth with the fear of abandonment.
[beat]
But we found the cracks in the lens.
And the cracks are where the air gets in,
where the fire learns to breathe.
[pause]
We flipped the script.
Not enough became capable.
Abandonment became safety.
Disappointment became enough.
Chaos became challenge.
Conflict became communication.
Unworthiness became courage.
[pause]
We stepped out of the automatic reactions.
We broke the resistance cycle.
[beat]
If we are not feeling neutral,
we are in resistance,
fighting the flame.
[pause]
So we choose the path of growth, joy, cause, choice, and surrender.
Surrender to the story.
Surrender to the truth.
That is how the old beliefs burn away.
[pause]
But as the light grows,
we must also look at the shadows.
The voices that whisper in the corners,
the ones that try to keep us small.
Our saboteurs.
The Judge, finding fault, throwing water on your spark.
The Pleaser, giving your heat away.
The Avoider, the Controller, the Victim.
[beat]
They are not the truth of who you are.
They are just the survival suits of a child.
[pause]
Quiet them.
And step into your Sage.
The wise, quiet center of the flame.
[beat]
And ask the only question that matters:
What is my stand?
[pause]
Because underneath every stand,
we are all driven by the same six fundamental needs.
Six fires we all seek to feed.
[beat]
We seek Certainty and Safety,
the comfort of a steady hearth.
We seek Uncertainty and Variety,
the spark of surprise and adventure.
[beat]
We seek Significance,
the need to feel unique, worthy, and special.
We seek Connection and Love,
the warmth of belonging and being close.
[beat]
And the needs of the spirit:
Growth,
expanding our capacity to learn and evolve.
And Contribution,
giving beyond ourselves to find our purpose.
[pause]
How we meet these needs is our stand.
How we choose to feed the fire.
[pause]
Because you have to know where you stand to lead.
You have to know your own heat to warm others.
[beat]
We spent a weekend in the territory of fear.
We watched how we lean in
and how we lean out of the fire.
[pause]
We saw fear as False Evidence Appearing Real.
Cold smoke rising in our heads.
[beat]
But we chose to play to win.
Not playing not to lose.
[pause]
We chose to risk.
To share.
To grow.
[beat]
We realized courage is not confidence.
You do not need confidence to start.
Enough courage over time creates the fire of confidence.
[pause]
And vulnerability is the only door.
Yes, you might get burned.
[beat]
But what we focus on expands,
and what we resist persists.
So we focus on our growth.
We focus on Face Everything And Rise.
[pause]
We step directly into the heat.
There is nothing in your life you are afraid to meet.
[pause]
And we build our leadership on four steady stones.
[beat]
First: Integrity.
The way you were designed to burn.
Not a perfect flame, but a steady one.
[beat]
And when the spark misses, remember:
There is no such thing as mistakes.
Only a mis strike of the match.
Only a retake.
A chance to strike once more,
to find your alignment in the dark.
[pause]
Second: Authenticity.
Your own unique color of flame.
Not copying the glow of others,
but feeding your own hearth.
[beat]
Finding your truth in the quiet reflection of the embers,
and naming it out loud, without hiding.
[pause]
Third: Source and Cause.
Knowing you are the one holding the match.
You are the friction that creates the heat.
[beat]
When another's behavior sparks tension in you, you do not blame.
You say:
This is the behavior I notice, and this is the interpretation I am bringing.
[pause]
You own the smoke you bring to the room.
Knowing the wind may blow, but you control your own flame:
How you think.
How you feel.
How you act.
[beat]
All events are neutral,
just dry wood waiting for a meaning.
[pause]
So you teach others where your fire line is.
You set your boundaries.
You take action.
You just do it.
[pause]
Fourth: Being Bigger Than Me.
Letting your individual flame reach outward.
Not just warming your own hands,
but joining a larger hearth.
[beat]
Standing with those who choose to burn bright,
until we are a bonfire of connection,
raising the heat for the whole room.
[pause]
We proved it.
We showed the world what happens when we work together.
That night at Rosario's.
[beat]
On that stage, in that crowded room,
building a sanctuary of sound.
Our final project together.
Our hearts on fire.
[pause]
We created the Purpose Project, rising in rhythm.
We chose to know our neighbor.
We chose connection, friendship, and community.
[beat]
We stood side by side, singing together,
live music bringing real impact,
proving that when our individual sparks gather,
we are stronger together.
[pause]
That night at Rosario's was the proof:
When we show up,
the world gets warmer.
[pause]
So we choose how we want to live our lives,
the five states of consciousness Gandhi mapped for the soul.
[beat]
Will we live in **Apathy**, cold ash, choosing not to care?
Will we live **Passive**, a smothered spark, hiding from the light?
Will we live in **Self Righteousness**, a wild fire of judgment, burning others to feel warm?
[beat]
Or will we live in **Courageous Action**,
stepping up,
acting **despite** the fear?
[pause]
And finally, will we live in **Nonviolence**?
The highest state,
the **cleanest** burn,
the ultimate way to live,
found only through the **cooling waters of forgiveness**.
[pause]
Taking accountability for the reactions we brought to the room.
Forgiving the stories we made up.
Choosing to burn with **love** instead of fear.
[pause]
We must become aware of our energy.
Notice the difference between the drainers and the generators in your life.
[beat]
The drainers are the things that exhaust us.
Toxic energy that leaves us feeling tired,
resentful,
and less than.
[pause]
But the generators are the things that boost our energy.
The actions and spaces that make us feel expanded,
bright,
and alive.
[pause]
And once we see the map of our energy,
we make a conscious choice about how we show up for others.
[beat]
Will you show up as a taker?
Perhaps slipping into survival,
clutching for warmth because we feel empty inside,
forgetting that we have our own light to offer?
[pause]
Or will you choose to be a giver?
A radiant hearth,
remembering your abundance,
actively sharing your light,
leaving everyone you touch expanded, bright, and alive?
[pause]
Enroll in your own life.
Choose to show up every single day.
Engage others.
Subscribe them.
Inspire them to subscribe to their own light.
[pause]
Listen to your intuition.
It is somatically felt.
Grounded.
Centered.
Like hot coals resting in the core of your body.
[beat]
Choose to be Big Me all of the time,
not just some of the time.
[pause]
Step into the Be, Do, and Have of your flame:
**Be:** grateful, radiant, courageous, connected, beautiful.
**Do:** surrender, take action, remain vulnerable.
**Have:** fun, gratitude, and courage.
[pause]
Because participation creates value.
Attitude creates experience.
Intention and action create results.
[beat]
This is the **fire journey**.
You are the **leader of your life**.
[pause]
Hold the stone in your hand.
Feel its weight.
[pause]
Take a breath.
[pause]
And **rise**.
