## Purpose Of This Document

This is a working outline for the planning conversation with Zorana. It is not final public copy.

The goal is to make the workshop feel grounded, meaningful, organized, and special. The workshop is not simply a long restorative yoga class. It is a three hour journey descending from the Crown to the Root, with restorative yoga, breathwork, meditation, reflection, and Reiki woven together.

## Big Picture Intention

The workshop introduces the seven chakras as a map of the body, the inner life, and the human journey from the spaciousness of the mind down into the deep grounding of the physical body. By moving from Crown to Root, we guide the nervous system from the noise of the day into profound, heavy rest.

The tone should be:
1. Grounded.
2. Spacious.
3. Reflective.
4. Accessible.
5. Spiritual without being vague.
6. Restorative without feeling passive.
7. Deep, but not emotionally overwhelming.

The central invitation:
We are not trying to fix or force the chakras into balance. We are listening through the body, noticing where there is flow, where there is holding, and where each center may be asking for care.

## Arrival and Opening, Twenty Minutes

### Laura's Introduction (Ten Minutes)
Welcome everyone into the space. Name that this is an experiential journey, not a lecture. 
Introduce the physiological container of the evening: talk briefly about the nervous system, how we hold stress in the body, and the role of the vagus nerve in bringing us back to a state of rest and digest. Frame the physical restorative postures as a way to create the safety the nervous system needs in order to let go.

Possible words:
Tonight we are going to move through the seven chakras as a map of the body and the inner life. We are not here to perfect anything or force anything open. We are here to listen, to rest, to notice, and to allow the body to show us where there may be steadiness, tenderness, holding, or quiet wisdom.

### Zorana's Introduction (Ten Minutes)
Zorana introduces the energetic container of the evening. She will talk about Reiki, the concept of energy, how it naturally runs through the body, and how blockages can form. She will explain how she will be moving through the room offering hands on or hovering Reiki to support the nervous system's return to balance.

Opening Question for the room:
What am I carrying into this room, and what would I like to set down for the next three hours?

## Crown Chakra, Sahasrara

Theme: Surrender, meaning, connection, unity, being held by something larger.
Element: Thought or spirit.
Body focus: Crown of head, vertical axis, whole body.

### Laura's Restorative Practice
1. Elevated Supported Savasana.
2. Supported Waterfall Pose.

### Breath Practice
I Am, Safe and Held Breath.

Let the breath be natural first. Nothing to deepen yet. As the body settles, invite students to silently hear "I am" with the inhale and "safe and held" with the exhale.

### Reiki Weaving
Zorana weaves in Reiki and her own energy wording for Crown. Laura holds quiet space.

## Third Eye Chakra, Ajna

Theme: Intuition, inner seeing, imagination, clarity.
Element: Light.
Body focus: Brow, eyes, forehead, inner gaze.

### Laura's Restorative Practice
1. Supported Child's Pose with Forehead Support.
2. Supported Seated Forward Fold with Forehead Support.
Teaching note: emphasize Third Eye contact in the first Child's Pose.

### Breath Practice
Quiet counting breath. Inhale for four, pause at the top of the inhale, exhale for six, pause at the end of the exhale. Invite students to imagine the breath coming in through the Third Eye and moving from the Third Eye down through the spine on the exhale.

### Reiki Weaving
Zorana weaves in Reiki and her own energy wording for Third Eye. Laura keeps the space quiet and simple.

## Throat Chakra, Vishuddha

Theme: Voice, truth, expression, listening.
Element: Sound.
Body focus: Throat, jaw, neck, mouth, ears.

### Laura's Restorative Practice
1. Cross Legged Seat with Jaw and Neck Self Massage.
2. Supported Reclined Throat Opener.

### Breath Practice
Hum Vaa during jaw and neck self massage.
AEIOU hum in Supported Reclined Throat Opener.
Teaching note: remember the ripple story. Vagus nerve as a current like a river. Humming sends ripples all the way down and back up.

### Reiki Weaving
Zorana weaves in Reiki and her own energy wording for Throat. Laura offers sound, then silence.

## Heart Chakra, Anahata

Theme: Love, grief, tenderness, connection, receiving.
Element: Air.
Body focus: Chest, upper back, lungs, arms, hands.

### Laura's Restorative Practice
1. Supported Heart Opener over Bolster.
2. Supported Side Lying Heart Twist.

### Breath Practice
Community heart breath. Inhale love from this community. Exhale love and compassion from the body into the room.

### Reiki Weaving
Zorana weaves in Reiki and her own energy wording for Heart. Laura gives more silence here.



## Solar Plexus Chakra, Manipura

Theme: Will, identity, boundaries, confidence, personal authority.
Element: Fire.
Body focus: Belly, ribs, diaphragm, center line.

### Laura's Restorative Practice
1. Supported Belly Down Twist.
2. Supported Prone Belly Rest, legs long.

### Breath Practice
Big belly breath, diaphragm breath.
Teaching note: as the belly expands, the diaphragm moves and can access the vagus nerve, sending signals to the brain that the body is safe. Bottom up somatic nervous system approach, body to brain.

### Reiki Weaving
Zorana weaves in Reiki and her own energy wording for Solar Plexus. Laura keeps cueing minimal.

## Sacral Chakra, Svadhisthana

Theme: Feeling, flow, pleasure, creativity, emotional permission.
Element: Water.
Body focus: Hips, pelvis, lower belly, fluid body.

### Laura's Restorative Practice
1. Supported Bridge on Bolster.
2. Supported Happy Baby.

### Breath Practice
Pelvic bowl breath.
Imagine the pelvis as a bowl of water. With each inhale, the water gently fills. With each exhale, the surface softens. There is nothing to force open here. We are practicing the possibility of flow.

### Reiki Weaving
Zorana weaves in Reiki and her own energy wording for Sacral. Laura keeps the language gentle and choice based.

## Root Chakra, Muladhara

Theme: Safety, belonging, steadiness, the right to be here.
Element: Earth.
Body focus: Feet, legs, pelvis, base of spine, weight.

### Laura's Restorative Practice
1. Supported Child's Pose.
2. Weighted Savasana.
Teaching note: Laura comes around and offers grounding sacrum pressure during Child's Pose, and places weight on the feet (e.g., sandbags or a bolster) during Weighted Savasana.

### Breath Practice
Weighted exhale breath.
Breathe in and feel the body gently expand. Breathe out and let the soles of the feet become heavier. Feel the floor meeting you through the feet, the pelvis, and the back body. Nothing to hold. Nothing to prove. Just the simple fact of being here.

### Reiki Weaving
Zorana weaves in Reiki and her own energy wording for Root. Laura holds an extended silence.

## Tea, Fruit, and Processing, Fifteen Minutes

### Return
Bring students back slowly from their profound rest.
1. Deepen breath.
2. Small movement in fingers and toes.
3. Roll to one side.
4. Return to seated.

### Nourishment and Grounding
Once seated, pour warm herbal tea and offer seasonal fruit. This is a critical physical grounding step. The act of eating, tasting, and hydrating helps bring the nervous system completely back into the present moment and allows the energetic work to settle before the mind tries to process it with words. Keep the space mostly quiet.

## Journaling, Fifteen Minutes

Offer one simple prompt. Provide paper and pens.

* What did I discover in the quiet tonight, and what do I want to carry forward with me?

## Final Closing and Goodbye, Ten Minutes

### Closing Circle
Students may share one word or reflection, but sharing is entirely optional.

Possible closing from Laura:
Thank you for taking this downward journey through the body with us. You may notice things continuing to move or settle after tonight. Be gentle with yourself. Drink water, rest tonight, and let the practice keep unfolding without needing to understand it all right away.

Zorana can add any final energetic advice for the evening.
