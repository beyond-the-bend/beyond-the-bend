# Jamie Initial Consultation Cheat Sheet

Date: Thursday, May 28, 2026

Length: About forty five minutes

Client: Jamie

Context: Jamie has been attending Laura's yoga classes for a few months and already knows Laura's teaching style, restorative yoga, and the general feel of Beyond the Bend.

## Intention For This Conversation

This is not the first full session. This is a warm, clear consultation to understand what Jamie is looking for, whether private work is a good fit, and what kind of support would be most helpful.

The goal is not to solve everything today.

The goal is to listen, clarify, explain the container, and decide on the next step.

## Before Jamie Arrives

Take two minutes to arrive in your own body.

1. Feel your feet.
2. Soften your jaw.
3. Let your breath drop.
4. Remind yourself: I do not have to perform. I only have to be present, clear, and kind.

Quick reminder:

Jamie already trusts you enough to ask. You do not need to prove yourself.

## Simple Flow

1. Arrival and welcome, five minutes.
2. What brought her here, ten minutes.
3. What she is hoping for, ten minutes.
4. Relevant body, health, and support context, ten minutes.
5. Explain the private work container, five minutes.
6. Pricing and next step, five minutes.

If she talks a lot, let the timing breathe. The most important pieces are what brought her here, what she needs, any important safety concerns, and the next step.

## Section Five: Explain The Private Work

Possible words:

The way I imagine this work is as a gentle, body based and reflective space. It is not therapy, and I am not here to diagnose or treat anything. What I can offer is deep listening, somatic awareness, restorative and mindful movement, breathwork, stillness, journaling, and support for beginning to hear what your body and inner life may be asking for.

Because it is private, each session can be shaped around what you are bringing. Some days may include more conversation and reflection. Some may include more restorative yoga, somatic movement, breath, Jin Shin Do inspired self care, journaling, or guided inner practices. If spiritual pieces ever feel appropriate, such as journeying or simple ritual, we would only include those with clear consent and if they feel right for you.

The intention is to help you feel steadier, more connected, and less alone inside what you are carrying.
