# Large Airbnb Property Market Research

Created June 17, 2026

## Research Scope

This first pass looked at large whole home Airbnb listings that could hold 16 or more guests, using a Friday to Sunday weekend search.

Search date used:

```text
September 11 to September 13, 2026
```

Guest count used:

```text
16 adults
```

Search type:

```text
Entire home
```

This is a useful comparison because it shows what large group properties are charging for a normal weekend booking. It does not prove annual income, occupancy, profitability, or exact demand.

## Biggest Immediate Finding

Airbnb showed 22 whole home results around Edmonton and Sherwood Park that could take 16 or more guests for the selected weekend.

Airbnb also showed 82 large whole home results across Alberta for the same search.

This suggests there is an active market for large group stays. The closest Edmonton area properties are mostly city homes, not retreat acreages. That matters because a seventy acre property with horses, land, retreat use, and a large house could be positioned differently from ordinary city group houses.

## What The Numbers Suggest

The observed Edmonton area range for large group whole home weekend stays was roughly:

```text
$1,085 to $3,590 CAD for a Friday to Sunday weekend
```

The most relevant higher end examples were:

```text
9 bedroom luxury retreat, sleeps 23:
$3,273 CAD

6 bedroom luxury retreat with hot tub, sleeps 16:
$3,590 CAD

8 bedroom listing, sleeps 22:
$2,874 CAD
```

This supports the idea that a large, well presented property that can sleep 20 to 25 people could potentially command a weekend rate in the $2,500 to $3,500 range, and possibly more if it is positioned as a retreat property rather than an ordinary house.

The strongest differentiators would be:

```text
Seventy acres

Horses

Large house

Retreat atmosphere

Hot tub or future contrast therapy

Fire circle

Outdoor gathering space

Yoga and wellness programming

Beautiful photography

Professional guest experience
```

## Important Cautions

These numbers are listing prices for one selected weekend, not proven income.

They do not show:

```text
Occupancy

Cleaning fees

Platform fees

Taxes

Insurance

Maintenance

Net profit

Annual booking pace

Whether the property is legally allowed to host large groups
```

The next research step would be to check availability calendars, compare several dates, and look for properties with many reviews, because reviews can indicate repeat booking volume over time.

## Early Planning Takeaway

The earlier placeholder of $2,500 to $3,500 for a weekend rental is not out of nowhere. It is now supported by visible Airbnb examples in the Edmonton area for large group homes.

However, it should still be treated as a planning range, not a forecast.

A more cautious early planning range would be:

```text
Conservative weekend rental:
$1,500 to $2,200

Strong large group weekend rental:
$2,500 to $3,500

Premium retreat style property with strong amenities:
$3,500 to $5,000 plus
```

The property being discussed could plausibly sit in the strong or premium range if the sleeping capacity, photos, amenities, safety, and guest experience are excellent.
