# Corporate Retreat Venue Market Research

Created June 17, 2026

## Research Scope

This research examined corporate meeting packages, team building experiences, rural retreat venues, overnight retreat centres, destination lodges, and premium corporate adventures in Alberta.

The purpose was to understand what companies currently pay, what comparable venues include, how corporate retreat products are structured, and what the proposed retreat property could reasonably charge.

## Biggest Finding

Corporate retreat pricing covers an unusually wide range because companies are buying very different products.

```text
Basic recreational team building:
$35 to $80 per person

Catered rural day retreat:
Approximately $89 per person

Hotel full day meeting package:
Starting around $119 per person

General corporate retreat planning range:
Approximately $200 to $500 per person per day

Premium guided corporate adventure:
$800 to $2,500 per person
```

The property should not compete as a low cost meeting room. Its strongest position would be a private, facilitated team experience combining productive meeting time with restoration, land, horses, fire, food, and eventually sauna or hot tub access.

## Direct Alberta Comparisons

## Prairie Gardens

Prairie Gardens is approximately twenty five minutes north of Edmonton. It hosts company picnics, team building days, and corporate retreats for groups ranging from five to three hundred people.

Published packages include:

```text
Family Adventure Barbecue:
Starting at $39 plus GST per person

Prairie Ploughman's Luncheon:
Starting at $69 plus GST per person

Fireroasters Fowl Feast:
Starting at $89 plus GST per person
```

The $89 package includes a private meeting space for the day, a four course meal, farm activities, team games, and a hands on activity with a take home item. It accommodates five to fifty guests.

Strategic takeaway:

A rural setting close to Edmonton can sell a complete corporate day for approximately $89 per person when the offer is primarily venue, food, and light team building.

This is an important pricing floor. Laura's retreat should charge more when it includes original facilitation, nervous system education, restorative practice, horse supported learning, or a more intimate premium experience.

## University Of Alberta Corporate Team Building

The University of Alberta offers recreational corporate team building in half day and full day formats.

Published half day pricing:

```text
$35 to $65 per person
```

Published full day pricing:

```text
$50 to $80 per person
```

The packages include instructors, facilities, equipment, staff, logistics, and organization. Food, beverages, and expert seminar leaders may cost extra.

The University states that these sessions are informal and do not include formal team development facilitation.

Strategic takeaway:

Companies can purchase a fun activity day for under $100 per person. Laura's offer must make the difference between recreation and meaningful facilitation very clear.

## Falcon Crest Lodge

Falcon Crest Lodge in Canmore offers corporate meeting packages for retreats, training, and strategy sessions.

Published pricing:

```text
Partial day meeting package:
Starting at $66 per person

Full day meeting package:
Starting at $119 per person
```

The full day package includes breakfast, lunch, two coffee breaks, and meeting room rental. Overnight rooms, upgraded meals, and extra equipment cost more.

Strategic takeaway:

A full day meeting package with food and a professional room begins around $119 per person before accommodation or specialized programming.

For twenty people, that equals at least $2,380. This does not include a custom retreat facilitator, horse experience, wellness programming, or private use of a large rural property.

## Mount St. Francis Retreat Centre

Mount St. Francis near Cochrane provides a simple, nonprofit retreat model with forty five bedrooms and meeting space for up to forty eight people.

Published group pricing:

```text
Day use with lunch and coffee:
$40 per person

Overnight stay with breakfast, lunch, dinner, coffee, and snacks:
$125 per person per day
```

Meeting rooms and audio visual equipment are included.

Strategic takeaway:

This represents the affordable end of the retreat centre market. The accommodation is simple, washrooms are shared, and the model is mission driven.

The proposed property should not use this as its target price, but it is a useful floor for the cost of basic group accommodation, meals, and meeting space.

## The Crossing At Ghost River

The Crossing is a private corporate retreat property approximately one hour from Calgary. It operates on 145 acres, has twenty seven guest rooms, and accommodates up to sixty people.

Its corporate offer includes:

```text
Meeting rooms

Accommodation

Onsite dining

Event planning

Audio visual equipment

Fast internet

Full property bookings

Nature activities

Fire pits

Leadership workshops

Facilitated team building
```

Prices are provided through custom quotes rather than published packages.

Strategic takeaway:

The closest established model is not merely a rental house. It is a fully supported destination where the venue, rooms, food, planning, activities, and facilitation can all be purchased together.

The proposed property could eventually follow a similar model on a smaller and more intimate scale.

## Metis Crossing

Metis Crossing is located approximately ninety minutes from Edmonton and operates on 688 acres.

Its corporate offer includes:

```text
Modern meeting spaces

Large conference capacity

Accommodation

Onsite catering

Outdoor gathering areas

Campfire sites

Team building

Cultural workshops

Land based learning

Private functions

Multi day executive education
```

The Cultural Gathering Centre can accommodate up to seven hundred people. The venue also offers lodge rooms, cabins, domes, food, spa services, and signature cultural experiences.

Corporate pricing is customized rather than publicly listed.

Strategic takeaway:

Corporate buyers are willing to leave Edmonton for a destination that offers a meaningful reason to travel. The property needs a distinct experience, not merely attractive rooms.

Horses, nervous system support, restorative practice, nature, and intimate private use could provide that reason.

## Elk Island Retreat

Elk Island Retreat is located near Fort Saskatchewan and markets its forest venue for leadership development, workshops, team building, and corporate gatherings.

The property offers onsite cabins, yurts, camping, recreational vehicle sites, and geodesic domes. Accommodation is charged separately from venue use.

Strategic takeaway:

An Edmonton area nature property can successfully sell both event space and onsite accommodation. This supports keeping corporate programming and lodging as separate visible components of the price, even when they are sold together as a package.

## Rimrock Banff

Rimrock Banff demonstrates the premium end of the corporate experience market.

Published corporate adventure pricing:

```text
Six hour guided Power Adventure:
$800 to $1,400 per person

Twelve hour Stellar Experience:
$2,000 to $2,500 per person
```

The experiences may include expert guides, equipment, meals, snacks, challenging outdoor activities, helicopter upgrades, professional video, and premium dining.

Strategic takeaway:

Corporate buyers do not evaluate every retreat according to ordinary venue rental prices. When an experience is distinctive, professionally held, and memorable, companies may pay hundreds or thousands of dollars per person.

The proposed property will not begin at Rimrock pricing. However, this shows that a high value corporate experience can become far more valuable than room rental alone.

## What Corporate Buyers Are Purchasing

Companies are generally purchasing one or more of five things:

```text
A place to meet

A shared team experience

Professional facilitation

Food and hospitality

Accommodation and destination value
```

The more of these needs a venue solves, the higher the price can become.

A meeting room alone is a commodity. A private property that manages the entire day and creates a clear organizational outcome is a premium service.

## Strongest Corporate Markets For Laura

Laura should not initially market to every company. Her strongest buyers are likely organizations whose staff carry substantial emotional or caregiving weight.

The most natural audiences include:

```text
Healthcare teams

Nurses and care providers

Nonprofit organizations

School leadership teams

Teachers and educational support staff

Social service organizations

Women led businesses

Women in leadership groups

Therapy and wellness practices

Human resources teams

Caregiver organizations

Small executive teams
```

Laura has a credible understanding of exhaustion, caregiving, responsibility, nervous system depletion, and the difficulty of creating space for oneself. That gives her a stronger position in restoration and sustainable leadership than in generic competitive team building.

## Recommended Corporate Offers

Suggested length:

```text
Three to four hours
```

Possible components:

```text
Arrival refreshments

Opening conversation

Gentle movement and nervous system regulation

Guided team reflection

Nature walk or mindful horse connection

Closing integration
```

Recommended pricing:

```text
Pilot:
$2,000 to $2,750 for up to twelve people

Established:
$3,000 to $4,000 for up to sixteen people

$125 to $175 each
```

The lower price assumes simple refreshments and limited horse involvement. The higher price should include stronger food, an assistant, horse supported activities, or Nordic amenities.


Possible components:

```text

Facilitated team intention

Restorative movement

Nervous system education
```

```text

$1,500 to $2,000 per person
```

It should be considered a future offer rather than a starting point.

## Venue Only Corporate Rental

Some companies will bring their own facilitator and only require the property.

Recommended pricing:

```text
Half day venue use:
$750 to $1,250

Full day venue use:
$1,250 to $2,000

One night exclusive property use:
$3,000 to $4,500

Two night exclusive property use:
$5,500 to $8,000
```

Food, accommodation setup, cleaning, horse activities, spa amenities, and Laura's facilitation should be added separately.

The venue only price must at least equal the income that would otherwise be earned through an Airbnb booking.

## Likely Costs

Corporate retreat gross revenue is not the same as profit.

Typical costs may include:

```text
Food and beverages

Cleaning and laundry

Guest supplies

Assistant facilitators

Horse professional

Chef or catering

Program materials

Insurance

Payment processing

Transportation

Photography

Property utilities

Setup and teardown labour

Sales and planning time
```

For an established full service retreat, a planning allowance of thirty five to fifty five percent of revenue for direct and operating costs is reasonable until the actual property costs are known.

Example:

```text
Full day retreat revenue:
$8,000

Estimated costs at forty percent:
$3,200

Estimated contribution before taxes, financing, and owner distribution:
$4,800
```

For a $15,200 overnight retreat:

```text
Estimated costs at forty five percent:
$6,840

Estimated contribution before taxes, financing, and owner distribution:
$8,360
```

These are planning examples, not researched profit margins.

## Strategic Conclusion

The earlier plan used $6,000 as an example full day corporate retreat. The new research supports keeping that number.

More specifically:

```text
Pilot full day:
$3,500 to $5,000

Established full day:
$5,500 to $8,000

Premium full day:
$8,000 to $12,000

Established overnight retreat:
$9,000 to $20,000

Premium overnight or executive retreat:
$20,000 to $36,000
```

Corporate retreats could become one of the property's strongest revenue streams, but Laura should sell a clear outcome rather than simply renting the venue.

The most credible starting promise is not that a team will be transformed in one day. It is that the team will have a thoughtfully held space to slow down, reconnect, reflect, and return to work with greater clarity.
