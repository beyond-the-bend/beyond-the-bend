The Sacred Journey: From Crown to Root

A Somatic Restorative Yoga and Reiki Workshop

Tuesday, July 28, 2026
6:00 PM to 9:00 PM
Rising Moon Studio

Held in presence by Laura Harvey, Somatic Guide, and Zorana Michalsky, Reiki Master.

The Sacred Journey: From Crown to Root is a three hour immersion designed to guide your awareness inward through the seven chakras, beginning at the crown and gently descending toward the root.

This workshop weaves together restorative yoga postures, somatic embodiment practice, Reiki, reflective journaling, warm herbal tea, and seasonal fruit. Rather than treating the chakras as something to fix, this experience uses them as a quiet map for listening to the body, the nervous system, and the subtle places where we may be holding tension, emotion, or fatigue.

As participants settle into fully supported restorative shapes, Laura will offer somatic guidance to help the body soften, release physical tension, and return to a steadier sense of presence. Zorana will guide awareness into the subtle body, offer reflections on the energy of each chakra, and provide Reiki to support deeper emotional release and integration.

This is a slow, spacious practice for people who are longing to rest, listen, soften, and feel more grounded within themselves.
