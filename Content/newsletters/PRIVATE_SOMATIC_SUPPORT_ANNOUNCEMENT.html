Private Somatic and Restorative Support

This work is for women who may be carrying too much, moving through change, recovering from burnout, navigating grief, caregiving, divorce, perimenopause, identity shifts, or simply feeling far away from themselves.

Each session is shaped around what is present. The work may include gentle movement, restorative yoga, breath awareness, body awareness, journaling, reflective conversation, meditation, nervous system support, and quiet integration.

It is not therapy, and it is not medical treatment. It is a grounded, body based, reflective space to help you soften survival patterns, listen inward, and begin to return to yourself with more steadiness and care.
