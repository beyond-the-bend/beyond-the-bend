## Confirmed Experience

1. The workshop is a three hour immersion.

2. The journey begins at the crown and gently descends through each chakra toward the root.

3. The chakra system is framed as a map for listening to the body and inner life, not as something to fix, force open, or perfect.

4. The experience includes restorative yoga postures, somatic embodiment practice, Reiki, reflective journaling, warm herbal tea, seasonal fruit, and quiet integration.

5. Laura guides restorative shapes, somatic awareness, breath, and body based listening.

6. Zorana Michalsky is named in the existing documents as the Reiki Master and co facilitator.

7. The tone of the room should feel grounded, spacious, reflective, accessible, spiritual without being vague, restorative without being passive, and deep without becoming emotionally overwhelming.

## Strongest Emotional Doorway

The strongest campaign doorway is not chakra education. It is the feeling of carrying too much and wanting somewhere quiet to lay it down.

The campaign should speak to people who are tired of thinking their way through tension, fatigue, restlessness, and emotion. It should invite them into a held evening where the body can soften, the mind can quiet, and the subtle inner life can be listened to without pressure.

## Brand Voice Direction

1. Speak like a real person beside the reader, not like a teacher above her.

2. Lead with shared human truth before details.

3. Keep spiritual language reverent but simple.

4. Avoid jargon, clinical framing, and high pressure marketing.

5. Use the language of rest, listening, support, steadiness, quiet, and coming home to the body.

6. Keep public captions in natural paragraph form, not stacked fragments.

7. Do not use dashes in final public facing copy.

## Campaign Pillars

1. The body has been carrying a lot.

2. The descent from crown to root as a return to grounding.

3. The chakras as listening places, not problems to solve.

4. Restorative yoga and Reiki as a held, quiet, embodied experience.

5. The intimacy of the room, the tea, the fruit, the journaling, and the slow pace.

6. Practical invitation around early bird timing, limited capacity, and simple registration.

## Suggested Platform Shape

1. Instagram feed should carry the reflective invitation, image led reminders, and carousel education.

2. Instagram stories should carry gentle countdowns, behind the scenes preparation, question prompts, and registration nudges.

3. Instagram reels should be simple and quiet, focused on Laura speaking, props being prepared, a journal prompt, or the image of the body softening into support.

4. Facebook should carry slightly longer captions, practical details, the event invitation, shareable reminders, and local community language for Sherwood Park and Rising Moon Studio.

5. No partner sharing packet is needed for Zorana because she already has her own promotional material.

## Visual Direction

The existing square Sacred Journey image clearly communicates the chakra journey and can anchor the campaign. It feels more mystical and saturated than the quieter Beyond the Bend palette, so the social campaign should balance it with softer caption graphics, calmer supporting posts, and simple layouts using warm sand, sage, blush, deep sage, and muted rosewood.

Recommended image system:

1. Use the existing Sacred Journey graphic as the primary recognition image.

2. Create simple quote or prompt cards for carousel slides.

3. Create story graphics with fewer words and more breathing room.

4. Avoid visuals that make the workshop feel like spectacle, fantasy, or performance.

5. Keep the body, the mat, the tea, the journal, and the quiet room present whenever possible.

## Risk And Accuracy Notes

1. Do not say all fourteen spaces are available.

2. Use language such as spaces have begun to fill or limited intimate gathering unless Laura provides an exact count.

3. Do not over promise healing, emotional release, chakra balancing, or spiritual transformation.

4. Do not make the offer feel like a lecture, certification, or technical chakra workshop.

5. Do not make the copy feel like a price list, though price can be included in social posts where registration clarity matters.

6. Do not use dashes in final public facing copy.

7. Use exact dates for early bird and regular pricing because the timing matters.
