# Sacred Journey Campaign Glossary

## Terms

### The Sacred Journey: From Crown to Root

The public name of the July 28, 2026 workshop at Rising Moon Studio. Use the full name in first mentions, then The Sacred Journey in later mentions.

### Somatic Restorative Yoga and Reiki Workshop

The public category for the offer. It signals that the workshop includes body based rest, supported postures, inward listening, and Reiki.

### Crown To Root

The defining structure of the workshop. The experience begins at the crown chakra and descends toward the root chakra, creating a felt movement from mind, meaning, and subtle awareness into grounding, steadiness, and return.

### Chakra Map

The seven chakras are used as listening places for the body and inner life. They are not framed as problems to fix, points to force open, or a system that participants need to understand before attending.

### Listening Through The Body

The core Beyond the Bend lens for this workshop. The practice invites participants to notice sensation, breath, emotion, steadiness, tenderness, holding, and quiet wisdom through embodied awareness.

### Held Container

The relational and practical atmosphere of the workshop. It includes intimate capacity, clear guidance, silence, journaling, tea, seasonal fruit, and the presence of both facilitators.
