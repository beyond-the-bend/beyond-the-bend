Private Somatic and Restorative Sessions with Laura

A quiet, steady space for people who have been carrying too much for too long.

Private somatic and restorative sessions offer one to one support for people who are feeling overwhelmed, exhausted, disconnected from themselves, moving through change, or needing support that feels slower, gentler, and more personal than a group class.

Each session is shaped around what is present for you. Depending on what your body and nervous system need, the work may include gentle movement, restorative yoga, breath and body awareness, meditation, somatic inquiry, reflective conversation, nervous system support, and quiet integration.

Laura brings many years of teaching yoga, restorative practice, meditation, breathwork, and body based inquiry to this work. Her approach is compassionate, grounded, and deeply respectful of each person’s pace. These sessions are not about fixing yourself or pushing through. They are about being listened to, supported, and gently guided back toward yourself.
