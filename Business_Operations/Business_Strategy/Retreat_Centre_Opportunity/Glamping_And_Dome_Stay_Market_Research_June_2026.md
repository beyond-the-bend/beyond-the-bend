# Glamping And Dome Stay Market Research

Created June 17, 2026

## Research Scope

This research looked at Alberta glamping domes, cabins, Nordic spa amenities, all inclusive stays, and retreat style accommodations.

The goal was to understand what a future dome, cabin, or tiny house on the proposed retreat property might charge, and what kind of annual income one or two units might generate.

## Biggest Finding

The Alberta market shows two clear pricing levels.

```text
Simple but beautiful private glamping:
$295 to $395 per night

Premium glamping with spa amenities, food, or a highly curated experience:
$475 to $725 per night
```

A dome on seventy acres with horses, privacy, a hot tub or sauna, and access to a larger wellness property could reasonably aim for the stronger end of the market once established.

## Glamp Good

Glamp Good operates on 160 private acres of boreal forest near Winfield, Alberta.

The business says it has hosted thousands of guests over five years. It currently offers two A frame cabins, five geodesic domes, a shower house, a Nordic sauna, a cookhouse, and access to a separate floating sauna experience.

Published starting prices:

```text
Tamarack Geodome:
$295 per night

Spruce Geodome:
$295 per night

Willow Geodome:
$295 per night

Aspen Geodome:
$295 per night

Alder Glass Geodome:
$395 per night

Fern A Frame Cabin:
$395 per night

Hawthorn A Frame Cabin:
$395 per night
```

Strategic takeaway:

Glamp Good shows that a private nature based dome does not need to be highly luxurious to command close to $300 per night. A stronger glass dome or cabin can begin around $395 per night.

Their statement that they have hosted thousands of guests is also a useful demand signal. It suggests that unusual nature accommodation can build meaningful repeat and destination demand in central Alberta.

## 350 Farms

350 Farms offers three private domes and positions the experience as all inclusive farm based luxury.

Their stays may include farm to table meals, breakfast, lunch, prosecco, seasonal treats, a wood fired sauna, and hot tub access.

Published starting prices:

```text
Reflections Dome:
$475 plus GST per night

Wander Dome:
$575 plus GST per night

Silent Sky Dome:
$725 plus GST per night
```

The Silent Sky dome sleeps up to five people. The listed rate begins with double occupancy, with extra guest fees for additional guests.

The premium package includes a private Nordic spa experience, a wood fired sauna, hot tub when weather allows, and delivered meals.

Strategic takeaway:

350 Farms proves that a carefully designed dome experience can charge far more than an ordinary cabin. The higher price is not only for the structure. It is for privacy, food, atmosphere, service, ritual, and spa amenities.

This is highly relevant to the proposed retreat property because the property could offer horses, land, yoga, somatic practices, fire, sauna, hot tub, and other optional experiences.

## Refuge Bay Luxury Camping

Refuge Bay is a four season Alberta glamping destination offering domes, cabins, cottages, and private sauna experiences.

Its dome options include:

```text
Ignis Dome:
Sleeps four
King bed
Private wood fired sauna
Optional wood fired hot tub rental

Aer XL Dome:
Sleeps eight
Two king beds
Private wood fired sauna
Optional wood fired hot tub rental
```

The domes include large panoramic windows, skylights, fire features, outdoor cooking, and private use of a barrel sauna.

Strategic takeaway:

Refuge Bay shows that larger domes can be designed for more than couples. A dome sleeping eight could support small families, women’s groups, retreat overflow, or premium group stays.

Its four season model also shows that a properly heated dome can generate income outside the summer season.

## Likely Pricing For The Proposed Property

### Simple Dome

This assumes a beautiful heated dome with a comfortable bed, electricity, private outdoor space, fire pit, and access to washroom facilities.

```text
$295 to $395 per night
```

### Premium Private Dome

This assumes better interior design, private washroom or easy private facilities, strong views, excellent privacy, horses nearby, and a hot tub or sauna experience.

```text
$395 to $550 per night
```

### Luxury Wellness Dome

This assumes a curated experience with a private hot tub or sauna, breakfast or food, optional yoga or somatic practice, horse connection, beautiful hospitality, and highly polished photography.

```text
$550 to $750 per night
```

### Retreat Overflow Dome

A dome could also have value beyond ordinary nightly rental. It could increase the sleeping capacity of workshops and retreats.

For example:

```text
Two extra guests paying $1,000 each for a retreat:
$2,000 additional retreat revenue

Four extra guests paying $1,000 each:
$4,000 additional retreat revenue
```

This means the value of a dome may come from both private bookings and increased retreat capacity.

## What Could Make This Property Stronger

The proposed property could have several advantages over a stand alone glamping business.

```text
The large house already creates a destination

Weekly classes bring people to the property

Retreat guests already know the brand

Private clients may extend their visit

Horses create a distinct emotional experience

The dome could be sold as retreat accommodation

The dome could be sold as a private romantic getaway

The dome could be part of a concierge wellness package

The property could offer optional yoga, somatic support, sauna, hot tub, food, and horse experiences
```

This allows one dome to serve several markets rather than relying only on ordinary Airbnb bookings.

## Suggested Development Order

The safest order would be:

```text
First:
Prove demand using the house

Second:
Confirm zoning, permits, utilities, septic, insurance, fire safety, and year round access

Third:
Add one high quality unit rather than several inexpensive units

Fourth:
Track bookings, operating costs, guest feedback, and seasonal demand

Fifth:
Add a second unit only after the first unit proves itself
```

The first unit should probably be designed as a premium wellness dome rather than a basic tent. A stronger unit has more potential to support the property brand and higher nightly pricing.

## Recommended Planning Number

For early financial planning, use:

```text
$425 per night
100 booked nights per year
$42,500 gross annual revenue
```

This is a reasonable middle planning scenario. It sits above basic domes but below the highly curated all inclusive spa experience.

For a stronger established unit with private sauna or hot tub and polished hospitality, use:

```text
$525 per night
120 booked nights per year
$63,000 gross annual revenue
```

These should remain planning scenarios until local zoning, construction cost, unit cost, and actual demand are confirmed.

## Key Strategic Takeaway

Glamping is not a small decorative addition. One strong dome could become a meaningful standalone income stream while also increasing retreat capacity and strengthening the identity of the entire property.

The market evidence supports:

```text
$295 to $395 per night for simpler Alberta glamping

$475 to $725 per night for premium spa or all inclusive glamping
```

A well designed unit on this property could reasonably target the middle or premium range because the surrounding experience would include land, horses, wellness, privacy, and access to a larger retreat destination.
