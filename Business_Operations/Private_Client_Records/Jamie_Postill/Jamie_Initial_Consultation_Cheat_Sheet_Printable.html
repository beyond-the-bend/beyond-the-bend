Jamie Initial Consultation Cheat Sheet

Date: Thursday, May 28, 2026

Length: About forty five minutes

Client: Jamie

Context: Jamie has been attending Laura's yoga classes for a few months and already knows Laura's teaching style, restorative yoga, and the general feel of Beyond the Bend.

Intention For This Conversation

This is not the first full session. This is a warm, clear consultation to understand what Jamie is looking for, whether private work is a good fit, and what kind of support would be most helpful.

The goal is not to solve everything today.

The goal is to listen, clarify, explain the container, and decide on the next step.

Before Jamie Arrives

Take two minutes to arrive in your own body.

  1. Feel your feet.
  2. Soften your jaw.
  3. Let your breath drop.
  4. Remind yourself: I do not have to perform. I only have to be present, clear, and kind.

Quick reminder: Jamie already trusts you enough to ask. You do not need to prove yourself.

Simple Flow

  1. Arrival and welcome, five minutes.
  2. What brought her here, ten minutes.
  3. What she is hoping for, ten minutes.
  4. Relevant body, health, and support context, ten minutes.
  5. Explain the private work container, five minutes.
  6. Pricing and next step, five minutes.

If she talks a lot, let the timing breathe. The most important pieces are what brought her here, what she needs, any important safety concerns, and the next step.

Opening Script

I am really glad you reached out, Jamie. Since this is a newer private offering, I thought we could use today as a gentle consultation. I would love to hear what is bringing you here, what kind of support you are hoping for, and whether this feels like the right fit for both of us.

There is no pressure to have it all figured out. We can just begin with where you are.

Notes:

Section One: What Brought Her Here

Choose a few questions. You do not need to ask all of them.

What made you reach out for individual support right now?

What has been feeling hardest lately?

Is there something specific you are hoping private sessions might help with?

When you imagine feeling more supported, what do you hope would feel different?

Is this more about your body, your emotions, your stress, your life circumstances, your sense of self, or a mixture of all of that?

Section Two: Her Body And Nervous System

What have you been noticing in your body lately?

Where do you tend to carry stress or tension?

How has your sleep, energy, or emotional steadiness been?

Are you moving through perimenopause, menopause, grief, burnout, caregiving, transition, or any other major life change right now?

Are there times when your body feels difficult to trust, inhabit, or listen to?

Section Three: Practice History And Preferences

You have been in restorative yoga with me. What has felt helpful in that class?

Are there parts of class that feel especially calming, nourishing, or useful for you?

Are there any parts of practice that do not feel helpful for you?

Have you done any breathwork, meditation, somatic work, Jin Shin Do, journaling, or guided journeying before?

Are you open to more reflective or spiritual pieces if they feel appropriate, such as journaling, guided inner work, ritual, or shamanic inspired journeying?

Section Four: Safety And Scope

Ask gently and simply.

Are there any injuries, health conditions, surgeries, medications, or physical limitations I should know about before we do private movement or restorative work together?

Are you currently working with a doctor, therapist, counsellor, physiotherapist, chiropractor, acupuncturist, or other practitioner?

Is there any kind of movement, breath practice, language, or stillness that does not feel safe or supportive for you?

Are there any topics or experiences you do not want to explore in this kind of setting?

If something feels uncomfortable in a session, how would you prefer to let me know?

Scope Reminder For Laura

Thank you for trusting me with that. I want to be really respectful of what you are carrying. Some of that may be important to hold with a therapist or medical practitioner as well. What I can offer here is body based support, grounding, gentle movement, breath, rest, reflection, and a place to listen inward safely.

Section Five: Explain The Private Work

The way I imagine this work is as a gentle, body based and reflective space. It is not therapy, and I am not here to diagnose or treat anything. What I can offer is deep listening, somatic awareness, restorative and mindful movement, breathwork, stillness, journaling, and support for beginning to hear what your body and inner life may be asking for.

Because it is private, each session can be shaped around what you are bringing. Some days may include more conversation and reflection. Some may include more restorative yoga, somatic movement, breath, Jin Shin Do inspired self care, journaling, or guided inner practices. If spiritual pieces ever feel appropriate, such as journeying or simple ritual, we would only include those with clear consent and if they feel right for you.

The intention is to help you feel steadier, more connected, and less alone inside what you are carrying.
