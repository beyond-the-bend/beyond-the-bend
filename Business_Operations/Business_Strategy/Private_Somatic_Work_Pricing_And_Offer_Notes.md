# Private Somatic Work Pricing And Offer Notes

Date: Thursday, May 28, 2026

## Current Jamie Founding Client Offer

Jamie is the first one on one client for this new private somatic work.

Laura has booked space for the next eight weeks and is hoping Jamie will choose an eight week container rather than single sessions only.

Recommended current offer:

1. Single private session, sixty minutes, one hundred twenty five dollars
2. Eight week private somatic support container, eight hundred dollars total
3. Payment option, one hundred dollars per week for eight weeks

The important framing is that the eight week option is not a sale or discount. It is the recommended container because Jamie's nervous system needs consistency, time, trust, and careful pacing.

The payment plan should still be framed as an eight week commitment, paid weekly if that feels easier.

Because your nervous system is carrying a lot, I think consistency will matter. The first few sessions would give us time to understand what feels supportive, what your body responds to, and how to move slowly without overwhelming your system.

My regular private session rate is one hundred twenty five dollars.

For an eight week container, the rate would be eight hundred dollars total. If it feels easier, that can also be paid as one hundred dollars per week.

## Why These Numbers Make Sense Now

One hundred twenty five dollars for a single private session is appropriate because this is not a casual yoga class. It is one on one, customized, trauma informed, somatic, restorative, and reflective support.

Eight hundred dollars for eight weeks brings the session rate to one hundred dollars. That feels accessible for a founding client without undervaluing the depth of the work.

Laura should not go lower than eight hundred dollars for this container. The work will require preparation, emotional steadiness, documentation, session planning, follow up reflection, and care around scope.

## Current Session Length

For Jamie and for this first version of the offer, sixty minutes is fair.

Longer is not necessarily better, especially for a nervous system carrying a high trauma load. A clean hour gives enough time for arrival, check in, restorative or somatic practice, and a gentle close without overworking the system.

Current simple offer:

Eight weekly sessions, sixty minutes each, eight hundred dollars total.

For Jamie, the goal is not to maximize income. The goal is to create a fair founding client container that gives the work enough time to breathe while still honoring Laura's energy and skill.
