# Private Somatic Support Pricing And Concierge Plan

Created June 16, 2026

## Current Situation

Private Somatic and Restorative Support is beginning to work as a real offer. There is already strong interest, with three people moving into or toward the initial private container.

The founding client rate was positioned as a small number of early spaces. That rate is:

```text
$800 for eight weekly private sessions
$100 per session
```

Because the room cost is $30 per session, this leaves approximately $70 per session before taxes, preparation, communication, scheduling, and the emotional labour of holding private work. This is too low for the offer to become a viable long term business pillar.

## Recommended Immediate Pricing Decision

Keep the first three founding clients at the $800 rate. This protects trust and keeps the beginning clean.

After those founding spaces are full, move to the regular rate:

```text
Eight week private container:
$1,200

Payment option:
$150 per week for eight weeks

Single private session:
$150
```

At this rate, after the $30 room cost, the session keeps approximately $120 before taxes and admin. This begins to make the work financially viable.

## How To Explain The Transition

Use clear, simple language. Do not apologize for the price increase.

```text
I opened a few founding client spaces at a reduced rate while this work was beginning. Those spaces are now full, and the regular rate for private somatic and restorative support is $1,200 for eight weekly sessions.
```

Alternative wording:

```text
The founding client rate is now full. I am currently opening a small number of additional private spaces at the regular rate of $1,200 for the eight week container, with a payment option of $150 per week.
```

## Future Pricing Path

This is the suggested path as the offer becomes clearer, stronger, and more established.

```text
Founding rate:
$800 for eight sessions
$100 per session

Next regular rate:
$1,200 for eight sessions
$150 per session

Established rate:
$1,600 for eight sessions
$200 per session

Premium rate:
$2,000 for eight sessions
$250 per session
```

The likely upper end for eight one hour private sessions, without additional between session support, is around $2,000.

## When To Raise The Rate

Move to $1,200 once the founding spaces are full.

Move to $1,600 when there are:

```text
A clear page
A clear intake form
Three to five strong testimonials
A repeatable session structure
Confidence explaining who this is for
A few people saying yes without needing lots of convincing
```

Move to $2,000 when there are:

```text
Consistent inquiries
A waitlist or limited openings
Clear transformation stories
Strong confidence holding the work
A sense that Laura's private time is becoming scarce
```

## Future Concierge Container

A future concierge version would not simply be eight private sessions. It would be a held, personal season of support where the client feels they have a guide, a rhythm, and a steady place to bring what is unfolding between sessions.

Possible name:

```text
Private Somatic Support Concierge Container
```

Possible price range:

```text
$2,400 to $3,000 for eight weeks
```

Possible structure:

```text
Eight private one to one sessions, sixty to seventy five minutes each

A personalized intake and intention setting process

A simple weekly practice plan based on what came up in session

Short personalized audio practices when useful, such as a grounding practice, body scan, restorative setup, or breath practice

Light between session support by email or voice note, with clear boundaries

Optional journaling prompts or reflective questions

A closing integration session or written summary at the end of the container
```

## Important Boundary For Concierge Support

The concierge version should feel personal and held, but it must not become unlimited emotional availability.

Possible boundary language:

```text
Between sessions, you may send one reflection, question, or voice note each week. I will respond within two business days with a short written or voice note reflection.
```

This keeps the container warm, ethical, and sustainable.

## Core Positioning

Use this phrase as a north star:

```text
A deeply held eight week container for women who are carrying too much and need a steady place to be supported, restored, and guided back to themselves.
```
