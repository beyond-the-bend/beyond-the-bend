1. Monday evening, Somatic Vinyasa
2. Tuesday morning, Rise and Restore
3. Tuesday evening, Restore and Reclaim
4. Wednesday morning, Somatic Hatha

Laura does not plan to run the Wednesday evening Embodied Flow class during the summer.

## Recommendation

The summer offer should feel like a gentle seasonal container, not just a list of class passes.

The strongest framing is:

Summer Online Practice, July and August

This gives people a way to keep practicing through travel, lake weekends, family visits, changing schedules, and the natural looseness of summer.

The message should be that summer does not need to be all or nothing. People can stay connected to their body, breath, and nervous system without needing to be perfect or attend the same class every week.

## Newsletter Business Section Draft

### From the Studio

As we move toward summer, I have been thinking about how easy it is for practice to become an all or nothing thing. We travel, host family, spend time outside, shift routines, and suddenly the quiet things that help us feel steady can drift to the edges. I do not think summer practice needs to be rigid, but I do think it can be deeply supportive to have a gentle rhythm to return to.

Through July and August, I will be continuing four online classes each week so you can practice from wherever you are. Monday evening Somatic Vinyasa, Tuesday morning Rise and Restore, Tuesday evening Restore and Reclaim, and Wednesday morning Somatic Hatha will all be available online. The Wednesday evening Embodied Flow class will pause for the summer.

If you are away, at the lake, visiting family, or simply wanting the ease of practicing from home, the online classes are there for you. You can use a drop in, 5 class pass, 10 class pass, or 20 class pass depending on what kind of summer rhythm feels realistic.

The invitation is not to make summer another thing to manage. It is simply to keep one thread of connection with your body, your breath, and the part of you that feels better when you keep showing up gently.
