## Instagram Caption 7

Use on Sunday July 12 at 9:30 AM.

Asset: Carousel titled From Crown To Root.

Most chakra practices move from the root upward.

For this workshop, we are moving in the opposite direction. We begin at the crown and slowly descend toward the root.

There is something meaningful about that movement. So many of us spend our days in the upper body, thinking, planning, anticipating, deciding, remembering, and trying to hold the whole shape of life together from the neck up.

The descent invites us back down.

Back into the breath. Back into the belly. Back into the pelvis, the legs, the feet, and the simple support beneath us.

By the time we arrive at the root, the invitation is not to have figured everything out. It is to feel a little more here.
