## Initial Support Direction

The first sessions should emphasize receiving support, slowing without stopping completely, and finding forms of embodiment that do not depend on athletic performance.

Patty may respond better to a gradual transition from movement into stillness than to being asked to become still immediately. Rhythmic, contained, low intensity movement may help her system recognize that slowing down is not the same as losing control.

Useful early themes may include support, enoughness, boundaries, choice, noticing effort, and differentiating discomfort from danger. Practices should invite curiosity rather than become another task for Patty to perform correctly.
